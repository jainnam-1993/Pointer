# Time and Timelessness

## The Nature of Time

We live as if time were real - past, present, future stretched out in a line.

But when we look carefully, what do we actually experience?

## The Investigation

Try to experience the past. You can have a memory, but the memory happens NOW.

Try to experience the future. You can have an expectation, but the expectation happens NOW.

Have you ever experienced anything except NOW?

## The Discovery

All experience happens in the present moment. The "past" is a present thought. The "future" is a present thought.

There is only ever now.

## The Implications

If there is only now, then:
- You cannot actually escape the present moment
- Your entire life has been this moment
- Liberation is not in the future; it is now or never

## Awareness and Time

Does awareness have a past? When we look, awareness seems timeless.

It doesn't experience itself as old or young. It doesn't experience itself as having a history.

Awareness is the eternal present in which time appears.

## Living Timelessly

This doesn't mean ignoring time for practical purposes. Plans, memories, and schedules serve functions.

But we don't live IN time. Time lives in us - in the timeless awareness that we are.

## The Freedom

The future cannot harm you because the future never comes. When it arrives, it is now.

You have only ever had to deal with now. And now is always manageable.
