# You Are the Answer

## The Search

We ask: What am I? What is real? What is the meaning?

We look for answers outside.

## The Discovery

You are the answer.

Not an answer to think about.

The living answer.

## The Irony

The one asking is the answer.

Awareness asking "What am I?" IS awareness.

## The End

When this is seen, questions end.

Not because all questions are answered.

Because the questioner is seen.

## The Living Answer

You don't have awareness. You ARE awareness.

You don't have being. You ARE being.

## The Peace

In this, there is peace.

Not because problems are solved.

Because the seeker is seen through.

## The Question

What if the question is already the answer?

What if looking is what you're looking for?

## The Pointer

You are the answer.

Not in the future. Now.

Look.
