# The Obvious

## The Blindness

The most obvious things are often unseen.

We look past them, looking for something hidden.

## The Example

Awareness is the most obvious thing there is.

Every experience confirms it.

Yet we miss it constantly.

## Why?

Because it's not spectacular.

Not hidden in a cave. Not at the end of a journey.

Here. Now. This.

## The Recognition

Recognition is seeing the obvious.

Not finding something new.

Seeing what was always here.

## The Shift

The shift is from "What am I looking for?"

To "What am I overlooking?"

## The Simplicity

Truth is obvious once seen.

"Of course. How did I miss this?"

Because you were looking for something else.

## The Invitation

What is most obvious right now?

Before any thought about it.

What is undeniably here?

## The Answer

Awareness. Being. This.

Obvious. Overlooked. Recognized.
