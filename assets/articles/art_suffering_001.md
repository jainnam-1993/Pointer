# The End of Seeking

## The Search

Most spiritual seekers are looking for something: enlightenment, peace, freedom, God.

This seeking can go on for decades, even lifetimes.

## The Irony

Here is the irony: seeking perpetuates the sense of lack. Every search implies something is missing.

But what we seek - awareness - is not missing. It is what is doing the seeking.

## The Recognition

Awakening is not finding what was lost. It is recognizing that nothing was ever lost.

The seeker dissolves in the recognition that what was sought was always here.

## When Seeking Stops

When seeking truly stops, there is not disappointment ("I didn't find it"). There is recognition ("It was never not here").

The search was a misunderstanding. But it was a necessary misunderstanding. Without seeking, we wouldn't have looked closely enough to see through seeking.

## The Caution

"Stop seeking" can become another instruction to follow. Now we're seeking the end of seeking!

This is why genuine teachers don't just say "stop seeking." They help you see that what you seek is what you are.

## The Life After Seeking

Life continues. Actions continue. Learning continues.

But the underlying desperation is gone. There is no longer a sense that something fundamental is missing.

## The Question

Are you seeking something that is absent? Or are you seeking something that is present but unrecognized?

Look carefully. What you seek is what is looking.
