# Awakening in the Digital Age

## The Challenge

We live in an age of unprecedented distraction. Our attention is constantly pulled by notifications, feeds, and alerts.

How do we recognize awareness when attention is fragmented?

## The Opportunity

The digital age also offers unprecedented access to teachings. Wisdom that was once available only in monasteries is now in your pocket.

## The Practice

**Using Technology Consciously:**

1. Before checking your phone: "What is aware right now?"
2. During scrolling: "Who is looking?"
3. After screen time: "Was awareness affected by that?"

**Creating Space:**

Regular periods without devices create space for natural settling.

Not as self-improvement, but as giving awareness a chance to notice itself without constant stimulation.

## The Insight

Here's the thing: awareness is present regardless of distraction level.

Even while checking email, what is aware of that activity?

Recognition doesn't require peaceful circumstances. It requires simply looking.

## The Paradox

You might recognize awareness while reading this on a screen, then immediately get lost in the next distraction.

That's fine. Recognition isn't maintained by the body-mind. It is simply noticed, again and again.

## The Invitation

Don't wait for ideal conditions. The digital environment is this moment's form.

Look: What is aware of these words? What is aware of wanting to check notifications?

That awareness is what you are.
