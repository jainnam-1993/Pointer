# The Power of Attention

## The Mechanism

Whatever we give attention to, we experience. Whatever we withdraw attention from, fades.

Attention is creative. It literally shapes our experienced reality.

## The Default

By default, attention follows habit:
- Worries pull attention to future
- Regrets pull attention to past
- Desires pull attention to what's missing

We experience accordingly.

## The Choice

We can direct attention. Not through force, but through intention and practice.

Where is your attention right now?

## The Spiritual Application

In spiritual practice, we redirect attention:
- From thought content TO what is aware of thought
- From experience TO what experiences
- From objects TO the subject

This redirecting is self-inquiry.

## The Discovery

When attention turns to its source, it discovers awareness.

This awareness was always here. Attention was just elsewhere.

## The Practice

Throughout the day, ask: Where is attention now?

Don't judge. Just notice. Then gently redirect to awareness itself.

## The Freedom

Mastery of attention is freedom. When you can place attention where you choose, you're no longer slave to automatic patterns.

Not suppressing what arises - but not automatically following it either.

## The Ultimate

Eventually, attention rests in awareness and doesn't need to be redirected.

This is effortless presence. This is home.
