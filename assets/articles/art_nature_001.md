# Nature as Teacher

## The Oldest Pointer

Long before human teachers, nature pointed to truth.

The vast sky. The flowing river. The ancient mountain. All teaching without words.

## What Nature Teaches

**The Sky:** Like awareness, it holds everything and is touched by nothing. Clouds pass; sky remains.

**Water:** It takes the shape of any container, yet remains water. Like awareness appearing as experience.

**Mountains:** Unmoved by weather, they exemplify the unchanging witness.

**Trees:** Rooted, present, not seeking to be elsewhere. Pure being.

## The Practice

Spend time in nature without agenda. Not hiking to get somewhere. Not exercising. Just being.

Let nature do the teaching.

## The Investigation

Look at a tree. Where does "tree" end and "air" begin?

Look at the sky. Can you find its center? Its edge?

Look at water flowing. Is it the same water moment to moment?

## The Recognition

Nature doesn't seek awakening. It already is what it is.

The human being thinking about awakening is one more expression of nature. What you seek is what you are.

## The Mirror

Perhaps nature seems peaceful because it reflects back our true nature - awareness without commentary.

When we are still enough, we recognize ourselves.

## The Invitation

Go outside. Not to learn something, but to unlearn the artificial sense of separation.

Let the natural world remind you what you are.
