# Radical Acceptance

## The Definition

Radical acceptance is accepting this moment exactly as it is, without any wish for it to be different.

It is not approval or liking. It is simply acknowledging what is.

## Why It's Radical

We spend most of our lives in subtle or not-so-subtle resistance:
- This shouldn't be happening
- I should feel differently
- They shouldn't act that way

This resistance is suffering.

## The Practice

Notice what you are resisting right now.

It might be subtle - a background complaint, a wish for different circumstances.

Now: Can you accept that this is what is?

Not forever. Not that it won't change. Just right now, in this moment, this is what is.

## What It's Not

Acceptance is not:
- Passivity (you can still act to change things)
- Approval (you can accept and still prefer differently)
- Giving up (acceptance often precedes effective action)

## The Mechanism

When we accept what is, resistance ends. Energy that was bound in fighting reality is freed.

Paradoxically, acceptance often leads to change. Resistance perpetuates.

## The Depth

The deepest acceptance is accepting the present moment unconditionally.

Whatever arises, "yes." Not "yes, but" or "yes, if." Just yes.

This is love. This is freedom.

## The Practice

Pick one thing you're resisting today. Practice accepting it completely.

Notice what happens when resistance ends.
