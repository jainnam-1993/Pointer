# The Art of Surrender

## What Surrender Is

Surrender is not defeat. It is not giving up.

It is the ending of resistance to what is.

## What Surrender Isn't

It's not passivity or weakness.
It's not accepting abuse or injustice.
It's not suppressing preferences or desires.

## The Mechanism

Resistance creates suffering. When we resist what is, we suffer. When we accept what is, resistance ends.

This is not the same as liking what is. We can accept without liking.

## The Practice

Notice where you are resisting right now.
Perhaps the body resists some sensation.
Perhaps the mind resists some circumstance.
Perhaps the heart resists some feeling.

Now: Can you allow it to be, exactly as it is?

Not forever. Just for this moment.

## What Happens

When we stop resisting, energy that was bound in resistance is released. There may be a sense of openness, spaciousness.

The circumstance may not change. But our relationship to it changes completely.

## Surrender to What?

Surrender is not to circumstances. Circumstances may need to change.

Surrender is to THIS MOMENT as it already is. Because it already is. Resistance doesn't change that - it only adds suffering.

## The Depth

The deepest surrender is surrender of the sense of being a separate controller.

"Not my will but Thine." Not because a God demands it, but because "my will" is an illusion.

When the separate controller surrenders, there is only flow.
