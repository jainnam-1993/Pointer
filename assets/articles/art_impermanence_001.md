# The Teaching of Impermanence

## The Observation

Look at any experience:
- Thoughts come and go
- Emotions arise and pass
- Sensations shift and change
- Circumstances transform

Nothing in experience is permanent.

## The Suffering

We suffer when we try to hold onto what changes.

We want pleasant experiences to last. We want circumstances to stay stable. We want relationships to remain unchanged.

But change is the nature of phenomena.

## The Freedom

When impermanence is truly seen:
- We stop grasping what will inevitably change
- We appreciate what is here while it's here
- We allow experiences to flow naturally

This is freedom within impermanence.

## The Question

But wait: If everything changes, is there ANYTHING permanent?

This is the key question.

## The Discovery

Look at your experience over time. Everything has changed - body, thoughts, feelings, circumstances.

But has awareness changed? Is the awareness here now different from awareness ten years ago?

The content has changed. The knowing has not.

## The Two Truths

Relative truth: Everything changes.
Absolute truth: Awareness - what you are - is unchanged.

Both are true at different levels.

## The Living

We can engage fully with the changing world, enjoying its flow.

While knowing that what we are is the unchanging witness of it all.

Loss is real at one level. At another, nothing is lost.
