# The Paradox of Effort

## The Dilemma

Non-dual teaching says: You are already what you seek. There is nothing to achieve.

But if we make no effort, we remain identified with the separate self. If we make effort, we strengthen the sense of a separate doer.

What to do?

## The Resolution

The paradox is only apparent. Let's look more closely.

## Stage One: Effort

Initially, effort is needed. We must turn attention toward awareness. We must inquire "Who am I?" We must sit in meditation.

This effort is made by the apparent separate self. That's fine. There's nothing else to work with.

## Stage Two: Recognition

Through effort, recognition happens. We see that what we are is awareness itself, not the separate self that was making effort.

## Stage Three: Resting

After recognition, effort begins to feel artificial. We see that awareness is always present. "Returning to awareness" is a concept - we never left.

## Stage Four: Effortless Being

Eventually, there is simply being. No one practicing, no practice needed. Just this.

## The Mistake

The mistake is skipping stages. Telling yourself "no effort is needed" before recognition has occurred keeps you stuck.

The other mistake is staying in effort after recognition has occurred.

## The Guidance

Make effort until effort becomes unnecessary. You'll know when this happens.

Until then, don't worry about the paradox. Just look: What is aware right now?
