# The Final Word

## After All Words

After all the teachings, all the articles, all the pointers:

What remains?

## The Simplest Truth

You are aware.

That's it. That's the final word.

## Everything Else

Everything else is commentary.

Useful commentary, perhaps. But commentary.

## The Invitation

Put aside all teachings. Forget all you've learned.

What is simply here?

Awareness.

## The Recognition

This awareness is what you are.

Not what you have. What you ARE.

## No More Seeking

When this is clear, seeking ends.

Not because you found something.

Because you recognized what was never lost.

## The Living

Now: Live.

Not as a seeker. Not as an awakened person.

As awareness, appearing as your life.

## The End

This is the end of teaching.

Not the end of learning. Not the end of growth.

The end of looking elsewhere.

You are here. You are aware.

That's all.
