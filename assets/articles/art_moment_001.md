# This Moment

## The Reality

This moment is all there is.

Not as a concept. As a fact.

Past is memory. Future is imagination. Both happen now.

## The Fullness

This moment contains everything.

Whatever is happening - all of it - is now.

## The Escape

We try to escape this moment:
- Into past (memory, regret)
- Into future (planning, worry)
- Into elsewhere (distraction)

But we never actually leave.

## The Recognition

Recognizing this moment is recognizing what is.

Not what was. Not what will be. What is.

## The Depth

This moment is not a brief flash.

It is eternal. Always now.

## The Practice

Notice: This is it.

This experience, right now, is your life.

Are you here for it?

## The Living

Living fully is living now.

Not after. Not when conditions change.

Now.

## The Question

Can you be completely here?

Not thinking about being here.

Actually here.
