# Look Now

## The Invitation

This is not about understanding. This is about looking.

Right now, look: What is aware?

## Not Later

Not after you've read more. Not after you've meditated more.

Now.

## The Looking

This looking is not with the eyes. Not toward any object.

It is attention turning to its source.

## What You Find

You might find: nothing to find.

No object called "awareness." No thing to grasp.

And yet: awareness is undeniable.

## The Recognition

This undeniable awareness is what you are.

Not an experience to have. The experiencer itself.

## The Immediacy

There is no path to what is already here.

Just look. Now.

## The Simplicity

The mind wants complexity. Steps. Time.

Truth is simpler. Look. See.

## Now

Right now: What is aware of these words?

Don't think about it. Look.
