# The Breath as Anchor

## The Connection

Breath happens now. It's always available as an anchor to presence.

Unlike thoughts, breath doesn't take you away from here.

## The Practice

Notice your breath. Don't change it. Just notice.

Breathing in, know you're breathing in.
Breathing out, know you're breathing out.

This is presence. This is meditation.

## Beyond the Breath

The breath is a doorway, not the destination.

Notice: What is aware of breathing?

The breath appears in awareness. Awareness is what you are.

## The Use

Use breath to return when lost in thought.

One conscious breath breaks the chain of unconsciousness.

## The Depth

Breath is life force entering and leaving.

In the gap between breaths: stillness.

What breathes? What is the life force?

These questions deepen the inquiry.

## The Simplicity

When everything seems complicated, return to breath.

It's always here. It's always now. It's always simple.

## The Invitation

Right now, notice your breath.

Let it be as it is.

What is aware of this?
