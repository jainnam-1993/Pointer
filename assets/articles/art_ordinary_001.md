# The Ordinariness of Awakening

## The Expectation

We imagine awakening as:
- Bliss and rapture
- Cosmic visions
- Superhuman powers
- The end of all problems

## The Reality

Awakening is recognizing what is already and always here.

It's ordinary. Not spectacular. Not special.

Just this, seen clearly.

## The Disappointment

Many seekers are disappointed when they glimpse awakening.

"That's it? Just this?"

Yes. Just this.

## The Depth

The extraordinary is hidden in the ordinary.

Simple presence is actually infinite. This moment is actually timeless. This awareness is actually boundless.

But it doesn't look special. It just looks like now.

## Why This Matters

If awakening were special, it would be something to achieve. If it's ordinary, it's what is already here.

The ordinariness is actually good news. It means you're not far from it. You're never far from it.

## After Awakening

Before enlightenment: chop wood, carry water.
After enlightenment: chop wood, carry water.

Nothing dramatic changes. Everything is revealed as already perfect.

## The Pointer

Look at this moment, exactly as it is.

Not looking for something special. Not trying to improve it.

Just this.

What's here?
