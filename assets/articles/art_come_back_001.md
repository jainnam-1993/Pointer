# Coming Back

## The Pattern

We recognize. We forget. We remember. We forget again.

This is normal. Don't make a problem of it.

## The Forgetting

Forgetting is not failure. It's the nature of the mind.

Identification with thought is a strong habit. It takes time to change.

## The Coming Back

Coming back is the practice. Not staying there. Coming back.

Every time you remember, you come back. That's enough.

## No Judgment

Don't judge the forgetting. That's more thought, more identification.

Just come back. Neutral. Simple.

## The Question

When you remember, ask: What is aware right now?

Don't answer with thought. Just look.

This is coming back.

## The Gradual

Over time, there may be less forgetting. Or not. It doesn't matter.

What matters is: when you remember, you come back.

## The Trust

Trust the process. Even during forgetting, awareness is present.

You never actually leave. You just don't notice.

## The Invitation

Right now, notice: awareness is here.

You came back. This is it.

When you forget, you'll come back again.

No problem.
