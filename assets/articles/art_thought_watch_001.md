# Watching Thoughts

## The Practice

One of the simplest yet most powerful practices: Watch your thoughts.

Don't try to stop them. Don't engage with them. Just watch.

## The Setup

Sit comfortably. Close your eyes.

Now, wait for the next thought like a cat watching a mouse hole.

What happens?

## Common Experiences

**Thoughts slow down:** When watched, thoughts often become less frequent.

**Thoughts speed up:** Sometimes awareness of thoughts triggers more thoughts.

**Gap appears:** You may notice space between thoughts.

**Identification happens:** You get lost in a thought stream, then "wake up" again.

All are fine. Just keep watching.

## What You're Learning

Through watching thoughts, you learn:

1. You are not your thoughts (you can watch them)
2. Thoughts arise uninvited (you don't control them)
3. There is awareness prior to thought
4. Thoughts have no power except what you give them

## The Key Insight

When you truly watch a thought, you're already free of it.

The watcher and the thought are in different dimensions. The thought cannot touch the watcher.

## The Integration

This practice can extend throughout the day.

Notice: A thought arose. What is aware of it?

This simple noticing breaks the automatic identification with thought content.

## The Caution

Don't try to maintain constant watching. That's exhausting and egic.

Just notice, when you remember, that thoughts are being watched.
