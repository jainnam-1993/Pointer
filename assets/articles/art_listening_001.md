# The Art of Listening

## The Usual Way

We usually listen with the mind already full:
- Agreeing or disagreeing
- Comparing to what we know
- Waiting to respond

This is not real listening.

## Deep Listening

Deep listening is receiving without resistance.

Not evaluating. Not comparing. Just receiving.

## Why It Matters

Teaching enters through listening. If the door is closed with thoughts, nothing enters.

Even the truest teaching bounces off a busy mind.

## The Practice

When receiving teaching:
1. Let thoughts settle
2. Don't try to understand yet
3. Receive the words fully
4. Let them land before responding

## Listening to Silence

The deepest teaching is silence. Can you listen to silence?

Silence is not absence of sound. It is presence before sound.

Listening to silence is listening to awareness itself.

## Listening to Life

Everything teaches. Can you listen to life?

The tree teaches. The child teaches. The difficulty teaches.

All of it is awareness speaking to itself.

## The Listening Self

Ultimately, listening is awareness aware of itself.

There is no listener separate from what is heard.

Just hearing. Just awareness.

## The Invitation

Right now, listen.

Not to specific sounds. To the listening itself.

What is that?
