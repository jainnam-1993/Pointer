# Non-Duality and Therapy

## Different Levels

Non-dual teaching and therapy work at different levels:

**Therapy:** Helps the apparent separate self function better, heal wounds, develop healthy ego.

**Non-dual teaching:** Reveals there is no separate self to begin with.

Both are valuable. They serve different functions.

## The Mistake

Some spiritual seekers reject therapy: "There's no self to heal."

But if there is unresolved trauma, it will continue to affect experience regardless of spiritual understanding. Understanding you are awareness doesn't heal attachment wounds.

## The Other Mistake

Some therapists reject non-dual teaching: "It's dissociation" or "It's spiritual bypassing."

But genuine recognition is not a defense mechanism. It is a direct seeing of what is actually true.

## The Integration

A mature approach integrates both:

1. Recognize what you fundamentally are (awareness)
2. Allow the body-mind to heal and develop as needed
3. Don't use either level to avoid the other

## When to Use What

**Therapy is useful for:**
- Trauma healing
- Relational patterns
- Developmental issues
- Depression and anxiety with clear psychological components

**Non-dual inquiry is useful for:**
- Existential questions
- The sense of fundamental lack
- The search for meaning
- Recognizing what you are

## The Bottom Line

You are awareness in which a body-mind appears. That body-mind may have wounds that benefit from healing.

Both can be true simultaneously.
