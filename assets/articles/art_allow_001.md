# The Art of Allowing

## The Practice

Allow whatever is here to be here.

Not changing. Not improving. Allowing.

## The Resistance

We constantly adjust experience:

Pushing away what we don't want.
Grasping what we do want.

This is exhausting.

## The Allowing

What if you let everything be?

Just for this moment.

## What Happens

When we allow:

Tension releases.
Struggle ends.
Peace emerges.

## Not Approval

Allowing is not approving.

You can allow and still prefer differently.

## The Depth

The deepest allowing is allowing yourself.

All of you. As you are.

## The Practice

Right now, can you allow?

Whatever thoughts. Whatever feelings.

Let them be.

## The Result

In allowing, there is peace.

Not because experience changes.

Because the fight with experience ends.
