# Wide Open

## The Quality

Awareness is wide open.

Nothing excluded. Everything allowed.

## The Resistance

We resist some experiences:

"Not this. Something else."

This is closure.

## The Opening

Can experience be met openly?

Not selecting. Not refusing.

Wide open.

## The Nature

Awareness is naturally open.

Closure is a contraction within openness.

Even closure is allowed.

## The Living

Living wide open:

Experiences come. Experiences go.

The openness remains.

## The Practice

Can you be wide open to this moment?

Whatever is here?

## The Fear

"But if I'm wide open, won't I be hurt?"

What you are can't be hurt.

Only what you think you are.

## The Freedom

Wide open is freedom.

Not defended. Not contracted.

Just here.
