# Dreams and Reality

## The Dream State

In a dream:
- There is a dreamer
- There is a dream world
- The dreamer experiences the world as real
- Waking up reveals it was all constructed

## The Question

How do you know you're not dreaming now?

The waking state feels real. But the dream state also felt real.

## The Teaching

Advaita uses dreams to question waking reality:

In the dream, where was the dream world? In awareness.
In waking, where is the waking world? In awareness.

Both appear in the same awareness.

## The Difference

You might say: "But waking is consistent. Dreams are chaotic."

True. But both are appearances in awareness.

## The Dreamer

Who dreams the dream? You as awareness.

Who "dreams" waking reality? Same awareness.

## The Awakening

We speak of awakening FROM the illusion of separation.

Just as we wake from a dream, we can "wake" to the dream-like nature of waking life.

## The Living

This doesn't mean waking life is unreal or unimportant.

It means we hold it lightly. Knowing it is appearance in awareness.

We can engage fully without being completely identified with the dream.

## The Practice

Tonight, before sleep, notice: I am about to enter a dream world that will feel completely real.

Tomorrow morning, upon waking, notice: I have entered another reality that feels completely real.

What is present in both?
