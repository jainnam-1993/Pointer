# The Difference Between Thinking and Awareness

## The Confusion

Most people live identified with their thoughts. They believe they ARE the voice in their head. This is the fundamental confusion that causes suffering.

## A Simple Experiment

Close your eyes. Wait for the next thought.

...

Did you notice the moment before the thought came? There was awareness, waiting, alert - but no thought.

This shows that awareness exists BEFORE thought.

## The Relationship

Think of it like a movie screen:
- The screen is awareness (unchanging, always present)
- The movie is thought (changing, coming and going)
- We usually focus entirely on the movie and forget the screen

## The Key Recognition

Thoughts come and go. What remains?

Between any two thoughts, something is present. It is not blank. It is aware.

This awareness is:
- Not created by thought
- Not dependent on thought
- Present whether thoughts are present or not

## Why This Matters

When we identify with thoughts:
- We suffer when thoughts are negative
- We fear when thoughts imagine danger
- We seek when thoughts imagine lack

When we recognize ourselves as awareness:
- Thoughts can come and go freely
- No thought can fundamentally disturb us
- We are already what we were seeking

## The Practice

Throughout your day, notice: "I am aware of this thought."

The one who is aware of the thought is not the thought.
