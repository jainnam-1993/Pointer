# The Direct Recognition

## The Way

The direct path needs no intermediary.

No teacher (ultimately). No practice (ultimately). No time.

Just recognition.

## How?

Turn attention to its source.

What is aware?

## Not Through Mind

Recognition is not intellectual understanding.

The mind can't know awareness. It arises IN awareness.

## The Immediacy

Recognition is immediate.

Not at the end of a process. Now.

## The Directness

Direct means:

No steps between you and truth.

You ARE truth.

## The Challenge

The mind wants steps, process, journey.

Directness threatens this.

## The Gift

The gift of directness:

No waiting. No earning. Now.

## The Invitation

Directly, now:

What is aware?

Not what you're aware of.

What is aware.
