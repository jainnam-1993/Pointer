# Spiritual Humility

## The Temptation

As we progress on the path, there's a temptation:

To think we know. To believe we've arrived. To feel superior.

This is spiritual ego.

## True Humility

True humility is not self-deprecation. It's recognition:

We don't know what anything is.
We don't know who we are.
We don't know what's happening.

This not-knowing is liberating.

## The Knowledge Problem

Every piece of knowledge is a concept. Concepts are not reality.

The more we know, the more we can become trapped in concepts.

## The Gift

Not-knowing opens us to what is.

When we don't know what something is, we see it fresh.

When we don't know who we are, we're free.

## The Practice

Practice saying "I don't know" - and meaning it.

What is awareness? I don't know.
What am I? I don't know.
What is this moment? I don't know.

Let not-knowing be your home.

## The Paradox

In not-knowing, there is a knowing.

Not conceptual knowledge. Direct knowing.

The mind doesn't know. But awareness knows itself.

## The Living

Living from humility:
- We stay open to learning
- We don't defend positions
- We can be wrong and it's okay
- We're always fresh, always beginners

## The Invitation

What do you think you know? Is it really true?

What is the gift of admitting you don't know?
