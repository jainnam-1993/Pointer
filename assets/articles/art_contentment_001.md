# The Nature of Contentment

## Conditional Contentment

We're usually content when:
- We have what we want
- Things go well
- The future looks promising

This contentment depends on conditions.

## Unconditional Contentment

Is there contentment that doesn't depend on conditions?

Yes. It is the natural state of awareness.

## The Discovery

Awareness itself is content. It doesn't seek anything.

It is complete exactly as it is, regardless of what appears in it.

## Why We Don't Feel It

We identify with the body-mind. The body-mind is always seeking.

When we identify as awareness, contentment is revealed.

## The Practice

Notice when you feel discontent. Something is missing. Something should be different.

Ask: What is aware of this discontent?

Is awareness discontent? Or does discontent appear IN contentment?

## The Shift

Contentment is not achieved. It is recognized.

When we stop believing we are the seeking self, contentment is here.

## The Living

This doesn't mean we stop having preferences or goals.

But beneath all activity, contentment remains. We act from fullness, not lack.

## The Invitation

Right now, without changing anything:

Is awareness content?

Look. Not the thoughts. Awareness itself.
