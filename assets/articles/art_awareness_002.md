# Where Is Awareness Located?

## The Question

We assume awareness is located in the head, behind the eyes. But let us investigate this directly.

## The Experiment

Feel your feet. Where is the awareness of your feet? Is it in your head, or is it exactly where the sensation is?

Now feel your hands. Where is the awareness of your hands?

Hear the ambient sounds. Where is the awareness of hearing?

## The Discovery

When we look carefully, we cannot locate awareness in any single place. It seems to be exactly where each experience is.

Or rather: each experience appears IN awareness, not the other way around.

## The Reversal

We usually think: "I am here (in the head), and experiences happen out there."

But actually: Awareness is not in any location. All locations appear in awareness.

## The Body

"But I feel like I'm in the body!"

Look more closely. Is the body inside awareness, or is awareness inside the body?

When you feel the body, the feeling appears in awareness. You never experience awareness inside the body - you experience the body inside awareness.

## The Implications

If awareness is not located anywhere:
- It cannot be harmed (harm requires location)
- It is not separate from anything (separation requires distance)
- It is not born or dying (birth and death require a location to begin and end)

## Continue Looking

Don't take this on faith. Keep looking until it becomes obvious from your own direct experience.
