# Nisargadatta Maharaj

## The Life (1897-1981)

Nisargadatta was a simple man - a cigarette seller in Bombay with a wife and children. He met his guru, Siddharameshwar Maharaj, relatively late in life.

His guru told him to attend to the sense "I am" and to trust nothing else. Nisargadatta followed this instruction with complete dedication and realized his true nature within three years.

## The Teaching

Nisargadatta's teaching is uncompromising:
- You are not what you think yourself to be
- Find out what you are
- Attend to the sense of "I am"
- The world is not; only you are

## The Approach

Unlike gentle teachers, Nisargadatta could be fierce. He would challenge visitors relentlessly, tearing apart every concept they held.

"You are not the body, you are not the mind, you are not the world. Find out what you are."

## The Method

His core instruction:
1. Notice the simple sense of being - "I am"
2. Attend to this sense without adding anything to it
3. Stay there, regardless of what thoughts arise
4. Eventually, the sense "I am" dissolves into pure awareness

## The Book

"I Am That" is one of the most influential spiritual books ever published. It consists of dialogues with visitors, compiled by Maurice Frydman.

## His Words

"The seeing of truth cannot be dualistic. It is neither the seer nor the seen. It is seeing itself."

"Wisdom tells me I am nothing. Love tells me I am everything. Between these two my life flows."
