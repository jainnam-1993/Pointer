# Just Sitting (Shikantaza)

## The Practice

Shikantaza means "just sitting." It is the essence of Soto Zen meditation.

There is no technique. No focus on breath. No visualization. No mantra. Just sitting.

## The Instructions

1. Sit with spine straight
2. Eyes slightly open, gazing downward
3. Breathe naturally
4. Don't try to achieve anything
5. Just sit

That's it.

## The Difficulty

This is the simplest practice and the most difficult. The mind has nothing to hold onto. It must eventually give up and simply be present.

## What It's Not

It's not blanking the mind. Thoughts will arise - that's fine.

It's not concentrating on anything. If attention focuses on something, let it relax.

It's not trying to achieve a state. There is no goal.

## What It Is

Just sitting is the expression of your already-awakened nature. You are not doing it to become something. You are simply being what you already are.

The Buddha didn't sit to become a Buddha. He sat as Buddha.

## The Master's Words

Dogen: "To study the Buddha way is to study the self. To study the self is to forget the self. To forget the self is to be enlightened by the ten thousand things."

## The Invitation

Try it. Sit for five minutes with no technique, no goal. Just sit.

What happens when you stop trying?
