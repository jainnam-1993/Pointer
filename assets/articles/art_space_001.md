# The Space of Awareness

## The Metaphor

Awareness is like space.

It contains everything.

Yet is touched by nothing.

## The Space

Consider the space in a room.

Objects come and go. Space remains.

## Awareness

Experiences come and go.

Awareness remains.

## No Boundary

Space has no boundary we can find.

Awareness has no boundary we can find.

## No Qualities

Space has no color, no shape.

Awareness has no color, no shape.

## Everything Appears

In space, objects appear.

In awareness, experiences appear.

## The Difference

Space is itself a concept, an appearance.

Awareness is not an appearance. It is what appearances appear IN.

## The Recognition

You are this space.

Not contained in space.

Being it.

## The Invitation

Can you sense the spaciousness of awareness?

Not as a thing. As what you are.
