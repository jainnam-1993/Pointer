# The Unchanging Witness

## Everything Changes

Look at your life. Your body has changed. Your thoughts change constantly. Your emotions come and go. Your circumstances shift.

Is there anything that has NOT changed?

## The Discovery

Throughout all changes, there has been awareness. The awareness that knows this moment is the same awareness that knew you as a child.

Not the same thoughts or feelings - those have changed completely. But the simple knowing presence has remained constant.

## The Test

Think back to a memory from childhood. Notice: there was awareness then. Notice: there is awareness now.

Has the awareness itself changed? Or only what appears in it?

## The Implication

If awareness has never changed:
- It is not affected by experience (experiences change, awareness doesn't)
- It is not aging (the body ages, awareness doesn't)
- It was not created and will not be destroyed (what changes can be created and destroyed; awareness doesn't change)

## The Identity Shift

We usually identify with what changes - the body, the mind, the personality.

What if we identified instead with what has never changed?

## The Recognition

This unchanging awareness is not something to achieve. It is what you have always been.

The changing phenomena you thought you were have always appeared IN what you actually are.

## The Question

What are you - the changing or the changeless? What do you want to identify as?

Look and see what is true.
