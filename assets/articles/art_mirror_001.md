# The Mirror of Mind

## The Analogy

Mind is like a mirror. It reflects whatever is placed before it.

The mirror doesn't judge reflections. Doesn't prefer beautiful to ugly. Simply reflects.

## The Lesson

Awareness is similar. It reflects experience without preference.

Thoughts arise - awareness reflects them.
Emotions arise - awareness reflects them.
Sensations arise - awareness reflects them.

## The Mirror's Nature

The mirror is not affected by its reflections.

Reflect fire - mirror is not burned.
Reflect water - mirror is not wet.
Reflect ugliness - mirror is not ugly.

## Awareness's Nature

Similarly, awareness is not affected by what appears in it.

Painful thought arises - awareness is not pained.
Pleasant feeling arises - awareness is not pleased.
Disturbing image arises - awareness is not disturbed.

## The Recognition

We usually identify with the reflections. "I am this thought." "I am this feeling."

The invitation is to recognize yourself as the mirror, not the reflections.

## The Practice

When something difficult arises, ask:

Is awareness disturbed by this? Or does this disturb only within awareness?

Notice: The mirror remains clear regardless of what it reflects.

## The Depth

Here the analogy breaks down. A mirror and its reflections are two things.

But awareness and its contents are not two. The reflections are made OF awareness.

Still, the pointing helps: You are that which reflects, not the reflections themselves.
