# The Spiritual Longing

## The Feeling

Deep inside, there is a longing. A sense of missing something. A call to something more.

This longing drives the spiritual search.

## What We Think We Want

We think we want:
- Enlightenment
- Peace
- Freedom
- God

But these are concepts. What do we really want?

## The True Object

The longing is awareness longing for itself.

We want to come home. And we've never left.

## The Paradox

The one who longs IS what is longed for.

But identified with the separate self, this is not recognized.

## The Recognition

When awareness recognizes itself, the longing ends.

Not because something was found. Because the illusion of separation was seen through.

## The Use of Longing

Longing is painful, but useful. It keeps us looking.

Don't suppress it. Let it drive you to recognition.

## The Fulfillment

The deepest fulfillment is not getting what you want.

It is recognizing that what you are has never been incomplete.

## The Question

What do you really want? Underneath all desires?

Is it something to get? Or something to recognize?
