# What You Resist Persists

## The Mechanism

When we resist something:
- We give it attention
- We add energy to it
- We keep it in place

Resistance perpetuates.

## The Evidence

Think of something you've resisted for years.

It's still here, isn't it?

## The Alternative

What happens when resistance stops?

Without the struggle, things can move, change, dissolve.

## The Practice

Notice what you're resisting right now.

Can you let it be, just for this moment?

Not forever. Not approving. Just... allowing.

## The Paradox

By not resisting, we don't become passive.

We become free to respond naturally rather than react automatically.

## The Depth

The deepest resistance is to this moment.

"This should be different." "I should be somewhere else."

## The Freedom

Allowing this moment completely is freedom.

Not resignation. Not defeat.

Recognition that what is, already is.

## The Inquiry

What are you resisting right now?

What happens if you stop?
