# Finding a Teacher

## Is a Teacher Necessary?

Many awaken without teachers. But a good teacher can save years of wandering, validate genuine insight, and point out blind spots.

## What to Look For

**Transmission:** Does their presence evoke something in you? Do you feel more present around them?

**Integrity:** Does their behavior match their teaching? How do they treat people?

**Accessibility:** Can you actually interact with them, or are they only available through media?

**Lineage:** Where did they learn? Who validated their teaching?

**Clarity:** Does their teaching make sense? Does it point clearly?

## Red Flags

- Claiming to be uniquely enlightened
- Sexual relationships with students
- Financial exploitation
- Encouraging dependence rather than freedom
- Dismissing questions or challenges
- Creating us-vs-them dynamics

## The Relationship

A good teacher-student relationship is not dependency. The teacher points; the student looks.

The goal is not to become like the teacher. It is to recognize what you are.

## Where to Find Teachers

**In person:** Local sanghas, meditation centers, retreats
**Online:** YouTube, podcasts, courses, live streams
**Books:** The written teachings of awakened beings

## A Caution

Don't shop forever. At some point, work with one teacher or tradition deeply.

And remember: the teacher points to what you already are. Don't get attached to the pointer.

## The Inner Teacher

Ultimately, the true teacher is awareness itself. All external teachers point to this.

Trust your own direct experience above any authority.
