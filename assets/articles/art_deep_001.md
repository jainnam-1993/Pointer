# After the Recognition

## The Initial Recognition

The initial recognition of awareness is often profound. There may be a sense of vast openness, of coming home, of "oh, this is it!"

But what happens next?

## The Common Pattern

For most people, the recognition fades. The old patterns return. There may be disappointment: "I thought I was done."

This is completely normal and does not mean the recognition was false.

## What Happens

The recognition reveals what we are. But the body-mind has decades of conditioning. This conditioning doesn't disappear instantly.

Thoughts continue to arise. Reactions continue to happen. The difference is: now there is awareness of them as arising.

## The Integration

Integration is the process of stabilizing in the recognition:
- Returning again and again to awareness
- Not identifying with thoughts about the process
- Allowing old patterns to unwind naturally
- Living from what has been recognized

## The Trap

The biggest trap is making a new identity: "I am awakened" or "I lost my awakening."

Both are thoughts. What you are is neither awakened nor unawakened - it is the awareness in which both concepts appear.

## The Invitation

Don't make awakening into a project. Don't make integration into a project.

Simply notice: what is aware right now?

This is not a step in a process. It is what is already so.

## The Good News

There is nothing to do and no one to do it. What you are has never been lost and cannot be found.

Everything else is just the show appearing in awareness.
