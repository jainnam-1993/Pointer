# Just Stop

## The Instruction

Stop.

Stop seeking. Stop trying. Stop figuring out.

Just stop.

## What Happens

When you truly stop, what remains?

Not nothing. Awareness.

This was here all along. Seeking obscured it.

## Why We Don't Stop

Stopping threatens the ego. If we stop, who are we?

The mind says: "Keep going. You're not there yet."

## The Seeing

See this. See that the seeking perpetuates the seeker.

And the seeker is the problem.

## The Practice

Right now: Stop.

Don't do anything with what arises.

Let it be.

## The Trap

"Stopping" can become another doing.

Don't TRY to stop. Just... stop.

If trying continues, that's okay. You'll stop eventually.

## The Recognition

In stopping, recognition is possible.

Not because stopping creates awareness.

Because stopping stops obscuring it.

## The Invitation

Can you stop? Right now?

Not tomorrow. Not when conditions are right.

Now.

What happens when you stop?
