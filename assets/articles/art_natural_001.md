# Your Natural State

## The Question

Before all conditioning, before all learning, what were you?

## The Answer

Awareness. Simply aware.

This is your natural state.

## The Covering

Over time, we learned:
- To identify with thought
- To believe we are separate
- To seek what we already are

These cover the natural state.

## The Uncovering

Awakening is not creating a new state.

It is uncovering the natural state.

Removing what was added, not gaining something new.

## The Qualities

The natural state is:
- Peaceful (not disturbed by thought)
- Content (not lacking anything)
- Present (not lost in time)
- Free (not bound by concepts)

## The Accessing

You don't access the natural state.

You ARE the natural state.

All else is added.

## The Return

Coming home to the natural state is:

Letting go of what you're not.

Relaxing into what you are.

## The Invitation

Right now, without adding anything:

What is your natural state?
