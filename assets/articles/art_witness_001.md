# The Witnessing Consciousness

## The Practice

One of the most direct practices is simply to be the witness.

Notice: There are thoughts. There are feelings. There are sensations.

And there is that which knows all of these. This knowing is the witness.

## The Instructions

Whatever arises, recognize: "I am not this. I am that which knows this."

Thought arises. "I am not the thought. I am aware of the thought."

Emotion arises. "I am not the emotion. I am aware of the emotion."

Sensation arises. "I am not the sensation. I am aware of the sensation."

## The Shift

As this practice deepens, there is a natural dis-identification from experience.

You no longer feel like you ARE your thoughts and feelings. You observe them.

This creates space. Reactions become responses. Drama becomes interesting phenomena.

## The Caution

The witness can become a subtle identity - "I am the witness."

Even this is seen by something. The witness is not what you ultimately are.

## Beyond the Witness

Eventually, the witness merges with what is witnessed. Subject and object collapse.

There is just THIS - awareness and its content as one seamless whole.

## The Use

The witness practice is useful:
- When identified with difficult experience
- When reactions are running you
- When you've lost perspective

Step back. Witness. Create space.

Then, if ready, let even the witness dissolve.
