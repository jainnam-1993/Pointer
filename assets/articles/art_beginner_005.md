# How to Use This App

## The Approach

This app is not about accumulating knowledge. It is about facilitating direct recognition. Use it as a support, not a substitute for looking.

## Daily Pointings

The pointings are not affirmations or positive thinking. They are invitations to look directly at your experience.

When you read a pointing:
1. Pause before reading
2. Read slowly, letting it land
3. Don't just understand it - look where it points
4. Sit with what you discover

## The Articles

Use the articles to clarify understanding. But remember: understanding about awareness is not the same as recognizing awareness.

After reading, always return to direct looking.

## Different Traditions

The app includes pointings from various traditions:
- **Advaita:** The path of inquiry and discrimination
- **Zen:** Direct pointing, often paradoxical
- **Direct Path:** Contemporary, precise investigation
- **Original:** Fresh pointers for modern life

All point to the same recognition. Use what resonates.

## The Inquiries

The guided inquiries walk you through structured investigation. Use them when you want support for deeper looking.

## Remember

The goal is not to become a better spiritual person. It is to recognize what you already are.

If you find yourself accumulating without looking, stop. Take a breath. Ask: What is aware right now?

This app is a finger pointing at the moon. Don't mistake the finger for the moon.
