# Is Awareness Personal?

## The Common Assumption

We assume awareness is personal. "My" awareness, "your" awareness. But is this actually true?

## The Investigation

Look at your current experience. There is seeing, hearing, thinking, feeling.

Now, is the awareness that knows these experiences shaped like you? Does it have your personality? Your history?

When we look carefully, awareness itself has no personal qualities. It is simply open, knowing presence.

## The Analogy

Consider space. The space in your room has no qualities of its own. It takes on the apparent qualities of whatever is in it.

Awareness is similar. It appears personal because it knows personal experiences. But awareness itself is not personal.

## The Implication

If awareness is not personal, then "my" awareness and "your" awareness are not two different things.

This is not a belief to adopt. It is a recognition that can be verified in direct experience.

## The Test

When you look at another person, notice: there is awareness here, knowing this experience of seeing them.

Is the awareness here fundamentally different from the awareness there?

## The Discovery

There is only awareness, appearing as many viewpoints. The separation is apparent, not fundamental.

This is why compassion is natural to awakening. There is no real "other" to exclude.
