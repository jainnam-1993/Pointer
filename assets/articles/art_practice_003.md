# The Art of Not-Doing

## The Problem with Doing

Most spiritual practice is doing: focusing attention, cultivating qualities, controlling the mind.

This keeps the sense of a separate doer in place.

## Not-Doing

Not-doing is not laziness. It is the recognition that awareness is already complete and doesn't need improvement.

## The Practice

Sit comfortably. Close your eyes.

Now: Do nothing.

- Don't try to meditate
- Don't try to be present
- Don't try to stop thoughts
- Don't try to achieve anything

Simply rest.

## What Happens

At first, the mind will look for something to do. This is its nature. Let it look.

Eventually, it settles. Not because you made it settle, but because it runs out of things to seek.

## The Discovery

In this settledness, notice: awareness is present. It was always present. It didn't need your help.

The sense of a "doer" was just another appearance in awareness. Awareness itself never does anything.

## The Paradox

"But I had to DO the practice of not-doing!"

Yes, initially. This is the paradox. We use effort to discover that effort was never necessary.

## Living From Here

Not-doing is not just for meditation. It is possible to live from this place of rest - acting in the world while knowing yourself as the unchanging awareness in which action appears.
