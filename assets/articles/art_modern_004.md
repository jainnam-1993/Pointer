# Awakening and Work

## The Question

Can non-dual recognition survive the demands of professional life? Or is it only for monks and meditators?

## The Good News

Awareness is present at work exactly as much as in meditation.

The deadline stress arises in awareness. The difficult colleague appears in awareness. The pressure, the boredom, the triumph - all in awareness.

## The Practice

**In Meetings:**
- Notice: There is awareness of this discussion
- Who is "having" this meeting?
- Can you remain as awareness while the personality engages?

**Under Pressure:**
- Notice the stress as sensation
- Ask: Is awareness stressed?
- Continue working from this recognition

**With Difficult People:**
- See: Awareness is here, knowing this person
- The same awareness that looks out of their eyes?
- How does relating change when you see this?

## The Transformation

Work doesn't become less important. It becomes less personal.

Excellence remains possible - even increases - when it's not contaminated by ego.

## The Warning

Don't use non-dual understanding to avoid engagement or responsibility.

"Nothing matters" is a spiritual cop-out. Everything matters, including your work.

## The Integration

Work is not separate from awakening. It is another form that awareness takes.

Your professional life is not an obstacle to recognition. It is an invitation.
