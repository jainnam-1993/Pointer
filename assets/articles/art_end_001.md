# The End of Suffering

## The Promise

Non-dual teaching promises the end of suffering.

What does this mean?

## What Ends

What ends is the suffering of being a separate self:
- The fear of annihilation
- The sense of lack
- The burden of being a separate person

## What Continues

Pain continues. Emotions continue. Challenges continue.

These are part of human life.

## The Difference

But suffering - the layer added by identification with the separate self - ends.

Pain without the "why me?"
Emotions without the "this shouldn't be"
Challenges without the "I can't handle this"

## The Recognition

When we recognize we are awareness:

The separate self that suffered is seen as appearance.

What we are doesn't suffer.

## The Paradox

We can still feel pain.

But there's no one to suffer it.

## The Living

Life includes difficulty.

But the weight of being a suffering self is gone.

## The Invitation

Notice any suffering now.

Ask: Who suffers?

Find that one.
