# The Limits of Language

## The Problem

We use words to discuss awakening. But words are symbols that point to experience. They are not the experience itself.

"Awareness" is a word. The reality of awareness is not a word.

## Why It Matters

Many seekers get stuck in understanding. They know all the terminology, can quote all the teachers, can explain all the concepts.

But understanding about truth is not the same as recognizing truth.

## The Analogy

Reading the menu is not eating the meal. Studying maps is not traveling the territory.

Words about awareness are not awareness itself.

## The Danger

Words can become obstacles when we think we "get it" because we understand the words.

"I am awareness" can become a concept that keeps us from the direct recognition it points to.

## The Use of Words

And yet, words serve a function. They point. They invite. They create conditions for recognition.

Like a finger pointing at the moon - the finger is not the moon, but it helps us look.

## The Practice

When you read or hear spiritual teaching, don't stop at understanding. Let the words point you to direct experience.

Then drop the words and look.

## Beyond Words

What you are cannot be captured in words. But it can be recognized directly.

Right now, without any words, what is here?

Look before the word "awareness." Look before any label.

What is this?
