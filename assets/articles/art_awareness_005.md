# Awareness and Sleep

## The Puzzle

In deep sleep, there is no experience of the world, body, or mind. Where does awareness go?

This question has fascinated seekers for millennia.

## Three States

Traditional teachings point to three states:
1. **Waking:** The world appears
2. **Dreaming:** A dream world appears
3. **Deep sleep:** Nothing appears

What is present in all three?

## The Common View

We usually say "I was unconscious" in deep sleep. But how do we know this? Because we remember NOT having experiences.

But who remembers? Something was present enough to later recall the absence of experience.

## The Deeper Look

Consider: if awareness completely disappeared in deep sleep, how would you know you had slept? There would be no continuity.

The sense of "I slept well" or "I slept poorly" indicates that something was present, aware of the quality of sleep.

## The Teaching

Awareness doesn't disappear in deep sleep. What disappears is the CONTENT of awareness - thoughts, perceptions, the world.

Awareness remains as pure being, without objects.

## The Implication

This means awareness is not dependent on objects to exist. It is self-existing.

In deep sleep, you taste your natural state - pure being without the disturbance of thought.

## The Recognition

You don't need to achieve contentless awareness. You experience it every night. The task is to recognize it while waking content is present.
