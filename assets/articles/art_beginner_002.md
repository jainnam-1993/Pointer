# Your First Inquiry

## Before We Begin

This is not a test. There is no right answer. We are simply going to look at something together.

## The Setup

Find a comfortable position. Take a few breaths. There's no need to achieve any particular state.

## The Inquiry

Notice that you are aware. You know that you exist. This knowing is immediate and undeniable.

Now, ask yourself: **What is aware?**

Don't try to answer with words. Just look.

## What You Might Find

- The mind might offer answers: "I am," "consciousness," "me"
- Notice these are thoughts ABOUT awareness, not awareness itself
- What is aware of these thoughts?

## The Discovery

When we look for awareness, we don't find an object. We don't find a location. We don't find a thing.

And yet, awareness is undeniably present. You could not be reading this without it.

## The Paradox

What you are looking FOR is what is LOOKING.

This is why self-inquiry is different from other investigations. The seeker and sought are not separate.

## Moving Forward

You don't need to understand this intellectually. The recognition happens in the looking, not in the thinking about it.

Come back to this simple question throughout your day:

**What is aware right now?**
