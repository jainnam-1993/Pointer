# Just This

## The Pointer

Just this.

Not this AND something else.
Not this PLUS enlightenment.
Not this IMPROVED.

Just this.

## What It Means

Whatever is here, right now, is it.

The thoughts arising. The sounds present. The sensations occurring.

Just this.

## The Search

We search for something more. Something special. Something missing.

But what if this, exactly as it is, is complete?

## The Recognition

Recognition is not adding something to this.

It is seeing this for what it is.

Full. Complete. Awareness.

## No Elsewhere

There is no enlightenment elsewhere.

If it's not here, it's not anywhere.

## No Later

There is no awakening later.

If it's not now, it's not ever.

## The Simplicity

All the teachings, all the practices, all the seeking:

They all lead here.

Just this.

## The Rest

In "just this," seeking ends.

Not because something was found. Because it was always here.

The search was for this, which was never not present.

Rest in just this.

Nothing else needed.

Nothing else possible.

Just this.
