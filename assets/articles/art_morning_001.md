# The Morning Mind

## The Fresh State

Upon waking, before the mind fully engages, there is a clarity.

The slate is momentarily clean.

## The Opportunity

This is an opportunity for recognition.

Before "I" and "my day" begin, what is here?

## The Practice

As you wake:
1. Don't open your eyes immediately
2. Notice: Awareness is present
3. Don't follow the first thoughts
4. Rest in simple being

## The Habit

Usually, we wake into a cascade of thoughts:
- What day is it?
- What do I have to do?
- How do I feel?

These pull us from presence into story.

## The Alternative

What if you woke into awareness first?

Let the thoughts come, but don't be swept away.

Notice the space in which they arise.

## Throughout the Day

The morning sets the tone.

Beginning in awareness makes returning easier.

## The Invitation

Tomorrow morning, try this.

Before anything: What is here?

Let the day begin from here.
