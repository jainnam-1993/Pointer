# The Myth of Enlightenment

## The Fantasy

We imagine enlightenment as:
- Permanent bliss
- No more negative emotions
- Perfect relationships
- All problems solved
- Superhuman abilities

This is the myth.

## The Reality

Awakening does not:
- Stop thoughts
- End preferences
- Make you superhuman
- Solve all problems
- Create permanent bliss

## What Changes

What changes is the sense of who is experiencing all this.

Thoughts continue, but there's no one thinking them.
Emotions continue, but no one is gripped by them.
Life continues, but no one is living it.

## The Personality

Personality doesn't disappear. Quirks remain. Preferences persist.

The difference: they're not taken as "me."

## The Suffering

Some suffering ends - the suffering of being a separate self.

Other discomfort continues - physical pain, emotional reactions, challenges.

But the relationship to discomfort changes.

## The Danger

The myth of enlightenment creates:
- Spiritual seekers who never feel "done"
- Teachers who pretend to be beyond human
- Students who project perfection

## The Invitation

Drop the myth. Look at what is actually here.

This moment, without the story of enlightenment, what is present?

Ordinary awareness. Ordinary life. Just this.

This is it. Not the myth. This.
