# The Power of Silence

## Beyond Words

In many traditions, transmission happens not through words but through silence.

Ramana Maharshi often sat in silence for hours. Visitors would come with questions and leave with answers - without words being spoken.

## Why Silence?

Words arise from silence and return to silence. Silence is prior.

What we are is prior to language. It cannot be spoken, but it can be communicated - directly, heart to heart, presence to presence.

## The Practice

Try sitting in silence. Not meditating on something. Just silence.

At first, the mind fills it with chatter. Let this happen. Don't fight it.

Eventually, silence reveals itself beneath the noise. The noise continues, but silence is recognized as the ground.

## The Discovery

In deep silence, the subject-object distinction softens. There is just awareness - neither in nor out, neither silent nor noisy.

This is the silence that includes all sounds.

## Living from Silence

The invitation is not to be silent all the time. Words serve their function.

But to live FROM silence. To let words arise from stillness and return to stillness.

Speaking from silence is transmission. Speaking from noise is just more noise.

## The Teacher's Silence

Great teachers transmit through presence, not just words. Their silence speaks.

We can all discover this silence. It is what we are.
