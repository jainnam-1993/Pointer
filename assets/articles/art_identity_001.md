# Who Do You Think You Are?

## The Assumed Identity

Without thinking about it, we know who we are:
- Name, age, gender
- Profession, relationships
- History, memories
- Personality traits

This is our assumed identity.

## The Question

But is this who you actually are? Let's investigate.

Your name: Were you this name before you were named? Would you cease to exist if your name changed?

Your history: You have memories. But are you the memories, or that which has them?

Your personality: Traits have changed over time. Something remains the same. What?

## The Layers

We can peel back layers:
- Body (changes constantly)
- Mind (thoughts come and go)
- Emotions (always shifting)
- Roles (you play many)

What remains when all these are set aside?

## The Core

At the core is simply: the sense of being, the sense "I am."

Not "I am this" or "I am that." Just I am.

## The Discovery

Even this "I am" is known by something. What is aware of the sense "I am"?

Here words fail. What remains is awareness itself - without qualities, without limits, without identity.

## The Living

This doesn't mean you stop using your name or forget your history.

But you know these are costumes, not what you are.

## The Freedom

When you no longer derive identity from changing things, there is freedom.

Who you are is not at stake. What can threaten awareness?

You can be anything - and nothing - because you are not your identity.
