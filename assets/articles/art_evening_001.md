# Evening Stillness

## The End of Day

As the day winds down, there is a natural settling.

Activity decreases. Stillness increases.

## The Opportunity

Evening is an invitation to rest as awareness.

The doing is done. What remains?

## The Practice

Before sleep:
1. Let the day go
2. Don't replay or plan
3. Simply be
4. Rest in awareness

## The Review

You can review the day from awareness:

Not judging. Just witnessing what happened.

"Today, this happened." That's all.

## The Release

Release any tension held in the body.

Release any thoughts held in the mind.

Let everything settle into stillness.

## The Sleep

Sleep comes naturally from this rest.

Not falling unconscious. Dissolving into awareness.

## The Cycle

Morning: Awareness awakens
Day: Awareness engages
Evening: Awareness settles
Night: Awareness rests

## The Invitation

Tonight, let yourself rest.

Not doing anything to earn rest.

Just resting. As awareness. Always already at rest.
