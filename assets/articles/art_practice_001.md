# Morning Recognition Practice

## The Opportunity

The moment of waking is precious. Before the mind fully engages, there is a window of simple presence.

## The Practice

When you first wake, before opening your eyes:

**1. Notice you are aware**
Don't do anything. Just notice that awareness is present.

**2. Ask: What is aware?**
Don't answer with words. Simply rest in the question.

**3. Notice the body**
Feel the body without labeling sensations. Let awareness saturate the body.

**4. Let thoughts arise**
Thoughts will come. Watch them arise in awareness. Don't follow them.

**5. Rest**
For just a minute or two, rest as awareness before beginning the day.

## The Effect

This simple practice sets a different tone for the day. You begin not as a person who must achieve, but as awareness in which a person appears.

## When You Forget

If you jump out of bed without remembering, that's fine. The practice is not about being perfect. It is about inclining attention toward what is already present.

## Throughout the Day

Return to this question whenever you remember: What is aware right now?

The morning practice is training for continuous recognition.

## The Goal

Eventually, there is no practice. There is simply the obvious presence of awareness, never lost.
