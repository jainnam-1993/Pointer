# Eckhart Tolle

## The Life (1948-)

Eckhart Tolle lived in a state of almost continuous anxiety and depression until his late twenties. One night, the thought arose: "I cannot live with myself any longer."

He suddenly noticed the duality in that thought - "I" and "myself." Which one was real?

In that moment of noticing, his identity as the thinking mind collapsed. He spent the next several years simply sitting on park benches, experiencing the peace that had been revealed.

## The Teaching

Tolle teaches the power of present-moment awareness:
- The present moment is all you ever have
- You are not your thoughts
- The pain body is accumulated emotional pain
- Awareness is the way out of suffering

## The Contribution

Tolle has brought non-dual teaching to millions through his accessible, non-religious language. He bridges Eastern wisdom and Western psychology in a way that reaches people who would never read traditional texts.

## Key Concepts

**The Pain Body:** The accumulated residue of past emotional pain that lives in us and periodically awakens.

**Presence:** The simple awareness of the present moment without mental commentary.

**Ego:** The identification with form - thoughts, possessions, roles.

## The Books

"The Power of Now" and "A New Earth" have sold millions of copies worldwide and been endorsed by Oprah Winfrey.

## His Words

"You are the sky. Everything else is just the weather."

"Life is the dancer and you are the dance."
