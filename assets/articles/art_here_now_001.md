# Here and Now

## The Only Location

Where does experience happen?

Not "there." Always here.

Here is not a location in space. It's where you are. Always.

## The Only Time

When does experience happen?

Not "then." Always now.

Now is not a moment in time. It's when you are. Always.

## The Recognition

You are always here. You are always now.

You have never been elsewhere or else-when.

This is what you are: here-now.

## The Mind's Elsewhere

The mind imagines elsewhere and else-when:
- There (where you're not)
- Then (when you weren't or won't be)

These are thoughts happening here-now.

## The Freedom

Recognizing here-now is freedom from:
- Regret (imagined else-when)
- Worry (imagined else-when)
- Comparison (imagined elsewhere)

All of that happens here-now. You can't escape.

## The Fullness

Here-now is not a small location and moment.

It is infinite. Everything appears in it.

Here contains all "there."
Now contains all "then."

## The Practice

Periodically check: Where am I? When is it?

The answer is always: Here. Now.

Rest in this.

## The Living

Living from here-now:

No reference to imagined other places and times.

Just this. Where you always are. When you always are.

Home.
