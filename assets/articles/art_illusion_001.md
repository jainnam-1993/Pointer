# Maya: The Nature of Illusion

## The Concept

Maya is often translated as "illusion." But this can be misleading.

Maya doesn't mean the world doesn't exist. It means the world is not what it appears to be.

## The Appearance

The world appears as:
- Solid, separate objects
- A container for events
- Something outside us

But is this actually the case?

## The Reality

When investigated:
- Objects are not solid (mostly empty space)
- Separation is not fundamental (everything connected)
- "Outside" is a concept (experience is always "here")

## The Veil

Maya is like a veil over reality. We see the veil and miss what it covers.

The veil is our concepts, our assumptions, our habitual seeing.

## Lifting the Veil

We don't destroy maya. We see through it.

When seen through, the appearance continues - but we're no longer fooled by it.

## The Play

Maya is also described as God's play (lila). Not deception but performance.

The world is a performance. Real as performance. Not real as what it appears to be.

## The Living

We can enjoy the play while knowing it's a play.

Engaging fully, not fooled. Playing the role, not believing we ARE the role.

This is freedom within appearance.
