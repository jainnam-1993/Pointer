# The Role of Grace

## The Paradox

Non-dual teaching often emphasizes that awakening cannot be caused or achieved. It happens by grace.

But we're also told to inquire, investigate, practice. Which is it?

## Understanding Grace

Grace is not a reward from an external God. It is the natural movement of awareness toward recognizing itself.

When conditions are right, recognition happens. We don't control when or how.

## The Role of Effort

Effort creates conditions. It turns attention toward truth. It wears away the habits that obscure recognition.

But effort cannot MAKE recognition happen. A farmer can plant seeds and water them. But growth happens by grace.

## The Balance

Neither pure effort nor pure surrender works:

- Pure effort becomes straining, trying to force what cannot be forced
- Pure surrender becomes passivity, waiting for something to happen

The skillful approach: effort that includes surrender.

## The Practice

Make effort with open hands. Do the practice without attachment to result.

Inquire deeply, then let go. Plant seeds, then trust.

## The Receiving

When recognition happens, notice: Did you DO it? Or did it happen?

Most people describe awakening as something received, not achieved. This is grace.

## The Gratitude

When grace is recognized, gratitude naturally arises. Not gratitude TO something, but gratitude AS the response of awareness to itself.

The dewdrop realizes it was always the ocean. Thank you seems inadequate, but it's what arises.
