# Non-Duality and Science

## The Convergence

Modern physics has discovered something mystics have always said: reality is not what it appears to be.

## The Discoveries

**Observer Effect:** Quantum mechanics shows that observation affects what is observed. Subject and object are not separate.

**Non-locality:** Particles remain connected regardless of distance. The universe is not made of separate parts.

**The Nature of Matter:** At the smallest scales, matter is not solid stuff but patterns of energy in a field.

## The Caution

We should be careful about claiming science "proves" non-duality. Science describes behavior; it doesn't reveal ultimate nature.

But the discoveries are suggestive. The old model of a universe of separate objects in empty space is no longer tenable.

## The Parallel

Both non-dual teaching and modern physics point to:
- The inseparability of observer and observed
- The fundamental wholeness of reality
- The appearance of separation being secondary to underlying unity

## The Difference

Science examines experience objectively (from the outside).
Non-dual inquiry examines the subject (from within).

Both approaches reveal the inadequacy of our usual view.

## The Integration

We don't need science to validate awakening. But for those with scientific minds, seeing that physics points in the same direction can be helpful.

## The Bottom Line

The investigation matters more than the conclusions. Look for yourself: Is there actually a boundary between subject and object?
