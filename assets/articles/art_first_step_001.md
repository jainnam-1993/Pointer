# The First Step

## Where to Begin

The journey of awakening can seem overwhelming. Thousands of teachings, practices, teachers.

Where do you begin?

## The Answer

Begin here. Begin now. Begin with what is.

Not with what should be. Not with what will be. What IS.

## The Practice

Right now, notice: You are aware.

That's it. That's the first step.

## Everything Else

All teachings point to this simple recognition. You are aware.

All practices refine this recognition. You are aware.

All awakening IS this recognition. You are aware.

## The Simplicity

Don't complicate it. The first step is the last step.

You are aware. You have always been aware. You will always be aware.

## The Journey

The journey is not from here to there. It is from here to here.

Deeper into what is already true.

## The Invitation

Take the first step. Notice you are aware.

Then take it again. And again.

This is the path.
