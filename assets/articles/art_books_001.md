# Essential Reading

## Advaita Vedanta

**I Am That** - Nisargadatta Maharaj
*The most important non-dual text of the 20th century. Dialogues with a Bombay sage.*

**Be As You Are** - David Godman (on Ramana Maharshi)
*Clear presentation of Ramana's teachings, organized by topic.*

**The Ashtavakra Gita**
*Ancient Sanskrit text. The most radical declaration of freedom.*

## Zen

**Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind** - Shunryu Suzuki
*Simple, profound. The essence of Zen practice.*

**The Gateless Gate** (Mumonkan)
*Classic koan collection with commentary.*

## Direct Path

**Being Aware of Being Aware** - Rupert Spira
*Precise, step-by-step investigation into the nature of awareness.*

**The Transparency of Things** - Rupert Spira
*Deeper exploration of the Direct Path approach.*

## Contemporary

**The Power of Now** - Eckhart Tolle
*Accessible introduction to presence.*

**Emptiness Dancing** - Adyashanti
*Honest, grounded teachings on awakening and integration.*

## How to Read

Don't just read for information. Read slowly. Stop when something resonates. Look where the words point.

One deeply read book is worth more than a hundred skimmed.

## Beyond Books

Remember: reading about awakening is not awakening. Books point; you must look.

Use books to support direct investigation, not to replace it.

## The Invitation

Choose one book that calls to you. Read it deeply. Let it work on you.

Then put it down and look: What is aware of these words?
