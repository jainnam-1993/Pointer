# Gratitude

## The Arising

When we recognize what we are:

Gratitude arises.

Not to something. Just gratitude.

## The Source

Gratitude for:
- Being
- Awareness
- This moment
- Everything

## Not Practiced

This gratitude is not practiced.

It is the natural response to recognition.

## The Fullness

When we see we are not lacking:

Gratitude for what is here.

Not asking for more.

## The Ordinary

Gratitude for ordinary things:

Breath. Awareness. Being.

Not taking them for granted.

## The Living

Living with gratitude:

Life becomes gift.

Not burden.

## The Practice

If gratitude is absent:

Look for what is here.

Really here. Now.

## The Recognition

In recognition, gratitude is natural.

Like the response to seeing the sunrise.

We didn't earn it. It's given.

Thank you.
