# Nothing More to Say

## The Teaching

All teaching points to this:

What you are.

Beyond that, nothing more to say.

## The Words

Words have been used.

Concepts have been pointed.

Eventually, words stop.

## The Silence

In the end: silence.

Not because nothing is true.

Because truth is not captured in words.

## The Living

After all words:

Living it.

Not thinking about it.

Being it.

## The Invitation

Put down the teachings.

Put down the concepts.

What remains?

## The Discovery

What remains is what was always here.

Before the first word was read.

After the last word is forgotten.

## The Simplicity

Just this.

Nothing more to say.

Just this.

## The End

And this is the end of words.

Not the end of truth.

The beginning of living it.
