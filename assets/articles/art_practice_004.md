# Inquiry in Relationships

## Relationships as Teachers

Relationships are powerful mirrors. They show us exactly where we are still identified with the separate self.

## The Triggers

When you feel:
- Hurt by someone's words
- Angry at their behavior
- Desperate for their approval
- Afraid of their rejection

These are invitations to inquire.

## The Practice

When triggered in a relationship:

**1. Pause**
Before reacting, take a breath. Notice the charge.

**2. Feel**
Where in the body is the reaction? What does it feel like?

**3. Ask: Who feels hurt/angry/desperate?**
Don't answer with "me." Look for this one who feels these things.

**4. Notice**
Can you find a separate self that is being affected? Or only awareness in which sensations appear?

**5. Return**
From this recognition, respond (or don't) to the situation.

## The Shift

When we relate from awareness rather than from the separate self, everything changes:
- We can hear criticism without defending
- We can love without needing
- We can disagree without attacking
- We can be close without fearing loss

## The Other

Here is the deeper recognition: the awareness in "you" and the awareness in "them" is not two.

Separation is apparent, not fundamental.

## The Invitation

Your most challenging relationship is your greatest teacher. Use it.
