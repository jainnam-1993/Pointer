# The Eternal Now

## The Teaching

Every spiritual tradition points to NOW. Why?

Because now is the only moment that exists. Past and future are thoughts happening now.

## The Proof

Try to experience the past. What happens? A memory arises NOW.

Try to experience the future. What happens? An imagination arises NOW.

You have never experienced anything except now. You never will.

## The Implications

If now is all that exists:
- The past cannot determine you (it's a present thought)
- The future cannot be feared (it's a present imagination)
- Your life is exactly one moment long

## The Depth

Here is the deeper point: Now is not a moment in time. It is what time appears IN.

Time is a concept. Now is direct experience.

## The Practice

Whenever you remember, check: What is happening NOW?

Not what was happening, what will happen, or what should happen.

Just: What is this moment's content?

## The Obstacle

The mind lives in time. It uses past and future to maintain the story of "me."

Coming to now threatens this story. Resistance is natural.

## The Freedom

In the now, there is no psychological suffering. Suffering requires time - regret, worry, complaint.

This moment, without story, is always bearable. Usually it's fine.

## The Question

What is here, before the next thought about past or future?

Look. This is it.
