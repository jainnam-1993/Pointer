# Effortless Awareness

## The Fact

Being aware requires no effort.

You don't TRY to be aware. You simply ARE.

## The Evidence

Are you making effort to be aware right now?

Or is awareness simply present?

## The Implication

If awareness is effortless:
- You can't lose it (no effort to maintain)
- You can't gain it (no effort to achieve)
- It's always here

## The Trap

We make effort to "stay present."

But presence doesn't need maintaining.

The effort IS the leaving.

## The Relaxation

Let all effort go.

What remains?

Awareness. Effortless. Always.

## The Practice

This is the non-practice.

Don't do anything.

Notice what is already here without doing.

## The Living

Living from effortless awareness:

Action happens. But not from effort.

From natural response.

## The Recognition

You are effortless awareness.

Not an awareness you have to maintain.

What you are.
