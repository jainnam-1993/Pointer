# The Gaps Between Thoughts

## The Stream

Thoughts seem continuous. An endless stream.

But actually, there are gaps.

## Finding the Gaps

Watch closely. Between one thought and the next, there is space.

A moment of no-thought. A gap.

## What's in the Gap?

In the gap, there is awareness. Just awareness.

No content. No problem. No self.

Pure presence.

## The Practice

Don't try to make gaps. Just notice them.

They're already there. We usually miss them because we're focused on thoughts.

## The Recognition

The gaps reveal what is always present.

Even during thoughts, awareness is here.

The gap just makes it obvious.

## The Peace

In the gap, there is natural peace.

Not created. Revealed.

This peace is your nature.

## Extending the Gap?

Don't try to extend the gap. That's another thought.

Just notice it. That's enough.

As you notice more, gaps may seem to expand. Or not. It doesn't matter.

## The Pointer

You are not the thoughts. You are what remains in the gaps.

Look.
