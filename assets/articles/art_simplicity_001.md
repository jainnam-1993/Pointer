# The Simplicity of Truth

## The Truth

The truth is simple: You are aware. Awareness is what you are.

That's it.

## The Complication

The mind doesn't like simplicity. It wants:
- More to understand
- More to do
- More to achieve

So it complicates.

## Why We Complicate

Complication keeps the seeker in business.

If it's simple, the search is over. The mind loses its project.

## The Return

Every teaching, at its core, returns to simplicity.

All traditions, all methods, all pointers: You are aware.

## The Practice

When things feel complicated, ask:

What is simply here, before complication?

## The Living

Simple doesn't mean easy. Living as awareness in a complex world is challenging.

But the truth itself is simple. We complicate it.

## The Invitation

Right now, without any philosophy:

What is here?

Let it be simple. Let it be obvious.

This is it.
