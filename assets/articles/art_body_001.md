# The Body as Doorway

## The Neglected Path

Much non-dual teaching focuses on mind and awareness. But the body can be a powerful doorway to recognition.

## The Reason

The mind can understand "I am awareness" and remain untouched. But when the body truly releases, understanding becomes embodied.

## The Practice

**Body Scan:**

Sit quietly. Feel into the body from head to toe.

But here's the key: Don't feel the body as an object. Feel it from inside, as experience.

Where exactly are the boundaries of "body"? Can you find an edge?

**Sensing:**

Choose one area - perhaps the hands. Feel the aliveness in them.

Now: What is aware of this aliveness? Is it separate from the aliveness?

**Relaxation Inquiry:**

Notice where you hold tension. As you breathe into it, ask: Who is holding?

Let the tension unwind without doing anything.

## The Discovery

When we feel the body from inside, we discover it is not solid matter. It is a field of sensation appearing in awareness.

The apparent boundary between body and environment becomes questionable.

## The Integration

Many people have recognition as awareness but remain disconnected from embodied experience.

Full awakening includes the body. Not as something owned by awareness, but as awareness itself in form.
