# What is Non-Duality?

## The Simplest Definition

Non-duality (Advaita in Sanskrit) means "not two." It points to the fundamental insight that subject and object, self and other, awareness and experience are not actually separate.

This is not a philosophy to believe in. It is a direct recognition available to anyone.

## Common Misconceptions

**It's NOT:** Saying everything is one blob, denying individuality, nihilism, or escapism.

**It IS:** Recognizing that the sense of being a separate self is a useful but incomplete view.

## The Core Insight

Right now, as you read these words:
- There is seeing (the screen)
- There is thinking (processing meaning)
- There is being (simple presence)

Where do YOU begin and where does experience end? When we look closely, we cannot find a boundary.

## Why Does This Matter?

The sense of being separate is the root of psychological suffering. We feel vulnerable, incomplete, searching. When this is seen through, there is:

- Natural peace (not dependent on circumstances)
- Spontaneous compassion (others are not truly "other")
- Freedom from seeking (what we sought was always here)

## The Beginning of the Path

Non-duality is not about adding new beliefs. It is about examining what we already assume to be true. Start with this question:

**What is aware of your current experience?**

Don't answer with a thought. Just look.
