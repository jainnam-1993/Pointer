# Complete As You Are

## The Sense

We feel incomplete. Something is missing.

This drives seeking.

## The Investigation

What is missing?

Can you name it? Find it?

## The Discovery

When we look, we can't find what's missing.

The sense of lack is itself a sensation in awareness.

## The Truth

Awareness is complete.

It doesn't lack anything.

You - as awareness - are complete.

## The Objection

"But I don't feel complete!"

The feeling of incompleteness is complete.

Nothing missing from the feeling itself.

## The Freedom

Recognizing completeness:

The search ends.

Not because something was found.

Because nothing was missing.

## The Living

Living from completeness:

Actions arise, but not from lack.

From fullness expressing.

## The Question

Right now, is awareness incomplete?

Is something missing from awareness itself?

Or only from the story about "me"?
