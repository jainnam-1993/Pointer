# Non-Dual Prayer

## The Question

In non-duality, there is no separate self and no separate God. So who prays to whom?

## Traditional Prayer

Traditional prayer assumes:
- A separate self (the one praying)
- A separate God (the one receiving)
- A gap to be bridged

## Non-Dual Understanding

In non-dual understanding:
- The one praying IS the one receiving
- Awareness is not separate from its source
- There is no gap

## So What Is Prayer?

Non-dual prayer is not request to an external God. It is:
- The movement of love within awareness
- Attention turning toward its source
- Openness to what is deeper than thought

## The Practice

Non-dual prayer might look like:
- Sitting in silence, receptive
- Offering gratitude without "to" or "from"
- Resting as awareness, open to grace
- Allowing words to arise from stillness

## The Paradox

From the non-dual view, prayer is awareness communing with itself.

Yet prayer can still be meaningful. The movement of devotion, even without separation, is part of the play.

## The Integration

You don't need to stop praying if you recognize non-duality.

Let prayer be what it wants to be. The words may change. The direction may shift.

What remains is the openness, the turning toward, the letting go.

## The Words

If words come, let them come.

"Thank you" with no one thanking no one.
"Help me" with no one asking no one.

The words are forms. The reality is formless love.
