# Working with Doubt

## The Problem

Doubt is natural on the spiritual path. The mind questions: "Is this real?" "Am I just fooling myself?" "Maybe it's all made up."

## The Two Types

**Healthy doubt:** Questions assumptions, prevents gullibility, keeps us honest.

**Neurotic doubt:** Never satisfied, questions even direct experience, prevents recognition.

## The Key

Healthy doubt questions the content of thought. Neurotic doubt questions even what is self-evident.

Ask: "Can I doubt that I exist?" Try it. The doubter must exist to doubt.

## Working with Doubt

When doubt arises:

1. Don't fight it. Fighting gives it power.

2. Inquire into it. Who is doubting? What is aware of doubt?

3. Notice: Doubt is another thought arising in awareness. Awareness itself doesn't doubt.

## The Fear Behind Doubt

Often, doubt masks fear. Fear of being wrong. Fear of change. Fear of losing the familiar self.

Recognizing the fear can release the doubt.

## The Use of Doubt

Doubt can be used constructively: "Is this thought true?" "Is this story accurate?" "Am I making this up?"

This kind of questioning leads to clearer seeing.

## The Resolution

Doubt cannot be answered at the level of doubt. More thoughts won't resolve it.

Resolution comes from seeing that doubt - like all thoughts - arises in awareness. What you are is prior to doubt.

Rest there.
