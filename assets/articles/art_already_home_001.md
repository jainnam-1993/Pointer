# Already Home

## The Seeking

We seek enlightenment as if it were somewhere else.

We travel to teachers, to retreats, to sacred places.

Looking for home.

## The Recognition

You are already home.

This. Here. Now. Awareness.

This is what you've been looking for.

## The Irony

The seeker looks everywhere except where they are.

Home was never lost. The looking was the leaving.

## The Return

Coming home is not going somewhere.

It's stopping. Noticing. Here.

## The Peace

The peace of home is always available.

Not because you've arrived somewhere.

Because you never left.

## What It Means

Being home means:
- Nothing fundamental is missing
- Nothing essential needs to change
- This, as it is, is complete

## The Life

From home, life continues.

Activity continues. Growth continues.

But without the homesickness of seeking.

## The Invitation

Can you see it? You are already home.

Not when you awaken. Now.

Welcome home.
