# Action in Inaction

## The Teaching

The Bhagavad Gita teaches: "See action in inaction, and inaction in action."

What does this mean?

## Action in Inaction

Even when we do nothing outwardly, the body-mind is active:
- Heart beats
- Breath breathes
- Thoughts arise
- Awareness witnesses

There is no true inaction. Action is always happening.

## Inaction in Action

Even when we are very busy:
- Awareness is still
- The witness doesn't move
- What we are is unchanged

In the midst of all action, there is stillness.

## The Recognition

We usually identify with action: "I am doing this."

The recognition: Actions happen in awareness. Awareness itself doesn't act.

## The Freedom

When we see this:
- Actions continue without "doer"
- Results happen without "reaper"
- Life flows without "me" managing it

## The Paradox

We can be fully engaged in action while knowing what we are is not acting.

Active and still simultaneously.

## The Living

This is not passivity. Engagement can be total.

But without the burden of being the doer.

Actions happen. Awareness witnesses. No problem.

## The Practice

In any activity, notice: Action is happening.

Ask: Is awareness acting? Or is action appearing in awareness?

See the stillness in the movement.
