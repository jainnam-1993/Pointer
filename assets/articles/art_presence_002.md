# Presence Is Not a State

## The Confusion

We seek presence as a special state.

Calm. Clear. Blissful.

## The Truth

Presence is not a state.

It is what is aware of all states.

## The States

States come and go:
- Calm states
- Agitated states
- Clear states
- Confused states

All appear in presence.

## The Recognition

Presence is here regardless of state.

Even in agitation, presence is present.

## The Freedom

This is freedom.

Not needing a particular state.

Being the awareness of all states.

## The Practice

Can you notice presence regardless of your state?

In calm: presence.
In chaos: presence.

## The Living

Living from presence:

States happen. You don't identify with them.

You are what knows them.

## The Question

Right now, whatever state you're in:

Is presence absent?

Or only the preferred state?
