# Love and Awareness

## The Discovery

As we rest in awareness, we often discover an unexpected quality: love.

Not personal love that depends on objects, but a boundless love that is the nature of awareness itself.

## Why Love?

When the sense of separation dissolves, what remains is not cold emptiness. It is warm intimacy.

Why? Because without separation, there is only connection. Love is the experience of non-separation.

## The Investigation

Think of someone you love. Feel the love.

Now: Is this love contained in you, or are you contained in it?

When we look closely, love doesn't seem to come from us. We seem to arise in love.

## The Misunderstanding

We usually think love is a feeling we have toward someone or something.

But real love is not directed at objects. It is the ground from which all relationship arises.

## The Practice

Rest in awareness. Notice if there is a quality of welcome, of acceptance.

This acceptance is not something you do. It is the nature of awareness - it accepts everything that appears.

This acceptance IS love.

## The Life

Living from this recognition doesn't mean we love everyone equally in personal terms.

It means we recognize that awareness - what we are - excludes nothing. All appears in it, welcome.

Personal love continues, but it floats on a deeper ocean of impersonal love.

## The Pointer

What you are is not only awareness. It is aware love. Knowing and loving are not two different things.
