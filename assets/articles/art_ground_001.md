# The Ground of Being

## The Question

What is the ground of all experience?

Everything appears. What do all appearances appear IN?

## The Discovery

All experiences - thoughts, perceptions, sensations - appear in awareness.

Awareness is the ground. The screen on which the movie plays.

## The Qualities

This ground:
- Is always present
- Has no form
- Cannot be seen (seeing requires separation)
- Is what we are

## The Figure and Ground

We usually focus on figures - the things that appear.

The ground goes unnoticed. But without ground, no figures could appear.

## The Recognition

Recognize the ground. Not as something separate from you.

AS you.

You are the ground on which all experience appears.

## The Stability

When we know ourselves as ground:
- Figures can change freely (we're not threatened)
- We don't try to fix appearances
- There's no need to prefer one experience over another

## The Metaphor

The ground doesn't mind what grows in it.

Flowers, weeds, crops - the ground receives all equally.

So too awareness receives all experience.

## The Living

Living as ground rather than figure:

Life happens on you rather than to you.

You are the space, not only what fills it.
