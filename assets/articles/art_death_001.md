# What Dies?

## The Most Important Question

In many traditions, contemplating death is considered essential. Not morbidly, but clearly.

What actually dies when the body dies?

## The Investigation

List what you consider yourself to be:
- The body
- The mind/thoughts
- The personality
- The memories
- The relationships

Now: Which of these is aware? None of them. They are all objects known by awareness.

## What Dies

The body will cease to function. Thoughts will stop. Personality will dissolve. Memories will end.

But awareness - was it ever born? Can something that was never born die?

## The Illusion

We fear death because we identify with what dies. We think we ARE the body, the mind, the personality.

When this identification is seen through, the fear of death loses its foundation.

## Not Denial

This is not denying the reality of physical death or the grief that accompanies loss.

It is recognizing that what you fundamentally are is not touched by physical death.

## The Practice

Ask yourself: "What was I before this body was born?"

Don't answer with philosophy. Look.

## The Freedom

When the fear of death is seen through, life opens up. We can live fully because we are no longer protecting a phantom.

We can face death - our own and others' - with clarity rather than terror.

## The Question

What will remain when everything you think you are is gone?

Look for yourself.
