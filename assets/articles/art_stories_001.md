# The Stories We Tell

## The Storytelling Mind

The mind is a storytelling machine. It creates narratives constantly:
- About who we are
- About what happened
- About what will happen
- About what should happen

These stories feel like reality.

## The Investigation

Take any story you're telling yourself right now.

It might be about a problem, a relationship, your life.

Ask: Is this story absolutely true? How do I know?

## The Recognition

Stories are made of thought. They may contain useful information.

But they are not reality itself. They are interpretations.

## The Suffering

We suffer not from what happens, but from our stories about what happens.

The event is neutral. The story makes it good or bad.

## The Freedom

Seeing stories as stories changes everything.

We can still think, plan, remember. But we're not trapped in the narrative.

## The Practice

When you suffer, look for the story. Find the thought that creates pain.

Ask: Is this thought absolutely true?

What is here without the story?

## The Reality

Beneath all stories: simple presence. What is, as it is.

Stories come and go. Presence remains.

Which are you?
