# Ramana Maharshi

## The Life (1879-1950)

At age sixteen, Venkataraman was sitting alone when the fear of death suddenly gripped him. Instead of running from it, he lay down and investigated: What dies?

In that investigation, he recognized that the body could die but what he truly was - awareness itself - could not. He had awakened spontaneously, without a teacher or practice.

He left home and went to Arunachala, a sacred mountain in South India, where he remained for the rest of his life.

## The Teaching

Ramana's teaching is simple:
- Ask "Who am I?"
- Don't answer with a thought
- Let attention return to its source
- Rest in what remains

## The Method

Self-inquiry (Atma Vichara) is his primary teaching:

When any thought arises, ask: "To whom does this arise?"
Answer: "To me."
Then ask: "Who am I?"
And return attention to its source.

## The Transmission

Ramana taught more through silence than words. People would sit in his presence and find their minds naturally settling into peace.

He never claimed to be a guru or to have disciples. He pointed only to the Self that everyone already is.

## The Legacy

Ramana's influence on modern non-dual teaching is immense. Teachers like Papaji, Mooji, and many others trace their lineage to him.

## His Words

"Your duty is to be, and not to be this or that. 'I am that I am' sums up the whole truth."
