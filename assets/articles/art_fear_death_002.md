# Freedom from the Fear of Death

## The Universal Fear

Fear of death underlies most other fears. It is the fear of non-existence, of ending, of losing everything.

## The Assumption

This fear assumes:
- You are the body-mind
- When body-mind ends, you end
- You were born and will die

## The Investigation

Were you born? Do you remember becoming aware?

Or was awareness simply here, and then a body-mind was noticed in it?

## The Discovery

Awareness was not born. It doesn't have a beginning.

Look for the edge of awareness, its start. Can you find it?

## The Implication

If awareness was not born, can it die?

Death is the ending of something that began. What never began cannot end.

## The Body

The body was born and will die. This is not denied.

But are you the body? Or does the body appear in what you are?

## The Freedom

When we recognize what we are is awareness - not born, not dying - fear of death loses its foundation.

The body will die. What we are will not.

This is not belief. It is direct recognition.

## The Life

This recognition doesn't make us careless about physical life.

It frees us to live fully, without the constant background fear of ending.

## The Test

Look at awareness right now. Does it seem like something that will end?

Or is it the timeless presence in which all ending and beginning appears?
