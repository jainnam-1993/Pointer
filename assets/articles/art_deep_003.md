# The Dark Night of the Soul

## The Territory

The "dark night" is a period of profound difficulty that can occur on the spiritual path. It feels like everything meaningful has been stripped away.

## The Experience

- Loss of previous spiritual experiences
- Absence of the sense of meaning
- Feeling disconnected from everything
- Depression that doesn't respond to usual remedies
- The sense that God or awareness has abandoned you

## The Meaning

The dark night is not a failure. It is often a deepening.

What's happening is that the ego's spiritual projections are being burned away. The idea of "my awakening" is dissolving. The seeker itself is dying.

This is painful because the ego experiences it as death.

## The Distinction

This is different from ordinary depression, though it can include depressive symptoms. The dark night specifically relates to spiritual seeking and the dissolution of spiritual identity.

If in doubt, seek professional support. There is no shame in getting help.

## Navigating the Dark Night

**Don't resist it:** Resistance prolongs it.

**Don't seek spiritual experiences:** The seeking is part of what's being burned away.

**Simple presence:** Just be with what is, without trying to change it.

**Physical care:** Take care of basic needs - sleep, food, movement.

**Trust:** This too shall pass. Others have navigated this territory.

## The Other Side

Those who have moved through the dark night often report a more profound, stable recognition than before.

What died was the seeker. What remains is what was always here.
