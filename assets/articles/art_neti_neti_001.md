# Neti Neti

## The Method

"Neti, neti" means "not this, not this." It is one of the oldest methods in Advaita Vedanta.

## How It Works

You are aware of thoughts. So you are not thoughts. Neti.
You are aware of feelings. So you are not feelings. Neti.
You are aware of body. So you are not body. Neti.
You are aware of world. So you are not world. Neti.

Whatever you can observe, you are not that.

## The Logic

If you can witness something, there must be a witness.

The witness cannot be the witnessed.

So negate everything witnessed. What remains is the witness - what you are.

## The Practice

Go through your experience systematically:
- Body sensations: Not this
- Thoughts arising: Not this
- Emotions present: Not this
- Sense of "me": Not this

What is left?

## The Discovery

When everything is negated, we expect nothing to remain.

But something does remain: awareness itself.

Awareness cannot be negated. It is what is doing the negating.

## The Trap

"Neti neti" can become dry, dissociative. "I am not anything."

This misses the fullness of what remains.

## The Fullness

What remains after negation is not empty void.

It is full awareness. Full presence. Full being.

Neti neti leads to Iti - "This!" The positive recognition of what you are.

## The Integration

Use negation to clear away false identification.

Then rest in what remains. Not nothing. Everything.
