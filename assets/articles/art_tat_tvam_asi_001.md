# That Thou Art

## The Declaration

"Tat tvam asi" - That thou art.

This is one of the great declarations (mahavakyas) of Vedanta. What does it mean?

## The Meaning

"That" refers to the ultimate reality - Brahman, awareness, the absolute.

"Thou" refers to you, the individual apparently reading this.

"Art" means: ARE.

You ARE that ultimate reality.

## The Misunderstanding

We might think: "I, as an individual, am the same as the universal."

But this misses the point. There is no individual separate from the universal to be unified with it.

## The Recognition

The recognition is:
- What I took myself to be (separate self) was never real
- What I actually am (awareness) was never separate
- There is only That, appearing as this

## The Investigation

Look at your sense of being an individual.

It's made of: thoughts, sensations, memories. All appearances in awareness.

Now look at awareness itself.

Is there a boundary to it? Does it have a center? Where does it end?

## The Discovery

Awareness is not limited to this body-mind. The body-mind appears IN awareness.

This awareness is not personal. It is universal awareness - That.

And you are That.

## The Living

This is not a belief to adopt. It is a recognition to verify.

When recognized, you don't become something new. You see what you always already were.
