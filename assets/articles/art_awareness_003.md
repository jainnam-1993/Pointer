# Does Awareness Have a Size?

## The Assumption

We typically feel like a small awareness looking out at a big world. But is this actually true?

## The Investigation

Look at the room you're in. Does it appear inside awareness, or does awareness appear inside the room?

Obviously, the room appears in awareness. You cannot experience anything outside of awareness.

## The Expansion

Now imagine outer space - vast, infinite. This imagination appears where? In awareness.

Everything you have ever experienced appeared in awareness. You have never experienced anything outside of awareness.

## The Question

If everything appears IN awareness, and nothing appears OUTSIDE awareness, what size is awareness?

It cannot be smaller than what appears in it.

## The Discovery

Awareness seems to have no size at all. It is not big or small. It is simply what knows experience, whatever the scale of that experience.

The "small me" feeling is just another experience appearing in this boundless awareness.

## The Shift

This recognition can feel disorienting at first. We are so used to feeling like a small point looking out at a big world.

But the disorientation passes, and what remains is a sense of spaciousness that is always present.

## The Practice

Throughout your day, notice: Does experience appear INSIDE awareness, or is awareness contained INSIDE experience?

Let this question work on you.
