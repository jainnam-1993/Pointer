# Ocean and Wave

## The Image

Imagine a wave in the ocean.

The wave seems separate. It rises, crests, falls.

But what is the wave made of?

## The Recognition

The wave is ocean. There is no wave separate from ocean.

The wave is a temporary form that ocean takes.

## The Application

You are like a wave. You seem separate. You rise, live, fall.

But what are you made of?

## The Recognition

You are awareness. There is no "you" separate from awareness.

You are a temporary form that awareness takes.

## The Fear

The wave might fear: "I will die when I crash on shore."

But the wave was always ocean. Nothing is lost.

## The Peace

When the wave recognizes it is ocean, fear ends.

Not because crashing won't happen. But because the wave knows its nature.

## The Application

When you recognize you are awareness, fear of death ends.

Not because dying won't happen. But because you know your nature.

## The Depth

The ocean analogy has limits too. Ocean is still a thing.

Awareness is not a thing. It is the knowing of things.

But the pointing helps.

## The Meditation

Feel yourself as a wave of awareness.

Rising and falling. Never separate from the ocean.

What remains when the wave subsides?
