# The Light of Awareness

## The Metaphor

Awareness is like light.

It illuminates experience.

Without it, nothing is known.

## The Light

Consider: How do you know anything?

There must be something that knows.

This knowing IS awareness.

## Self-Luminous

A light doesn't need another light to be seen.

Awareness doesn't need something else to be known.

It is self-luminous.

## Everything Visible

By this light, everything is seen:
- Thoughts
- Feelings
- Perceptions
- The world

All lit by awareness.

## The Light Itself

But the light itself is often unnoticed.

We see what it illuminates.

We overlook what illuminates.

## The Turning

Turn attention from objects to the light.

What illuminates this experience?

## The Discovery

The light has no color, no form.

Yet without it, nothing appears.

## The Recognition

You are this light.

Not seeing it. Being it.
