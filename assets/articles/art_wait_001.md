# The Art of Waiting

## The Usual Response

Waiting is usually experienced as:
- Frustration
- Boredom
- Time wasted
- Something to get through

## The Opportunity

What if waiting is an opportunity for presence?

Traffic, lines, delays - invitations to stop.

## The Practice

When waiting:
1. Notice the impatience
2. Ask: What is impatient?
3. Feel the body
4. Notice awareness

The waiting becomes meditation.

## No Longer Waiting

When present, you're no longer waiting.

You're not in between here and there.

You're here. Fully.

## The Shift

The mind says: "I'm waiting for ___"

Presence says: "There is just this moment"

## The Everywhere

Waiting happens everywhere:
- For food to cook
- For someone to reply
- For circumstances to change

Each is an opportunity.

## The Liberation

Liberation from waiting is not getting what you want faster.

It's not needing the next moment to be different from this one.

## The Invitation

Next time you wait:

Stop. Feel. Notice.

What is this moment, exactly as it is?
