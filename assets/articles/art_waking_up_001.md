# Waking Up vs Growing Up

## The Distinction

Spiritual teacher Ken Wilber points to an important distinction:

**Waking up:** Recognizing what you are (awareness)
**Growing up:** Developing as a human being (maturation)

These are different dimensions. Progress in one doesn't automatically mean progress in the other.

## Waking Up

Waking up is the recognition of awareness, the seeing through of the separate self, the discovery of what we fundamentally are.

It can happen suddenly. It is not a gradual development.

## Growing Up

Growing up is psychological development. Moving from egocentric to ethnocentric to worldcentric. Developing emotional intelligence. Working through trauma. Gaining wisdom.

It takes time. It is gradual.

## The Problem

Some awaken without growing up. They see clearly what they are but remain emotionally immature, relationally unskilled, ethically inconsistent.

We've seen this with teachers who abuse students.

## The Integration

Full human flourishing requires both:
- Recognizing what we are (waking up)
- Developing as a human being (growing up)
- Showing up in the world (waking down)

Awakening is not a bypass for human development. It is complementary to it.

## The Invitation

Don't use awakening to avoid growth. Don't use growth to avoid awakening.

We are called to both: to recognize our nature AND to develop our humanity.

This is the full path.
