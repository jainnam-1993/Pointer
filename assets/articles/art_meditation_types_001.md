# Types of Meditation

## The Variety

There are hundreds of meditation techniques. Understanding the differences helps us choose skillfully.

## Concentration (Samatha)

**What it is:** Focusing attention on a single object (breath, mantra, image)
**What it develops:** Calm, stability, focus
**The aim:** Tranquility

## Insight (Vipassana)

**What it is:** Observing experience to see its nature (impermanence, no-self)
**What it develops:** Wisdom, clarity
**The aim:** Liberation through understanding

## Self-Inquiry

**What it is:** Investigating the nature of the "I"
**What it develops:** Direct recognition of awareness
**The aim:** Liberation through recognition

## Just Sitting (Shikantaza)

**What it is:** Sitting with no technique or goal
**What it develops:** Natural presence
**The aim:** Expression of awakened nature

## Open Awareness

**What it is:** Resting as awareness, allowing all experience
**What it develops:** Recognition of what you are
**The aim:** Stabilizing as awareness

## Which to Choose?

Different practices serve different functions:

- Agitated mind? Concentration can help settle it.
- Stable but stuck? Inquiry can cut through.
- Straining too hard? Just sitting can release effort.

## The Caution

Don't collect techniques. Find what resonates and go deep.

And remember: all techniques are temporary. Eventually, practice gives way to simply being what you are.
