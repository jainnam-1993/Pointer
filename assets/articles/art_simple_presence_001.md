# Simple Presence

## The Essence

Strip away everything:
- No technique
- No goal
- No effort
- No understanding

What remains?

Simple presence. Just this. Here, now.

## What It Is

Simple presence is:
- Awareness, aware
- Being, just being
- Life, living itself

No elaboration needed.

## What It Includes

Simple presence includes everything:
- Thoughts (arising in presence)
- Feelings (appearing in presence)
- Sensations (known by presence)
- World (present to presence)

Nothing is excluded. All appears in this.

## The Nothing Special

This is nothing special. Presence is always here.

We tend to ignore it because we're looking for something dramatic.

But what you're looking for is the looking itself.

## The Access

How to access simple presence?

Stop seeking it. It's here.

Notice: Aware. Present. Just this.

That's it.

## The Living

Simple presence is not a state to maintain. It is what is.

Life continues. Drama happens. Presence remains.

Not as something you have, but as what you are.

## The Pointer

Right now, before any effort or understanding:

What is here?

Just this. Simple presence. Nothing missing.
