# Do You Know You Exist?

## The Question

Before any other knowing: Do you know you exist?

Not think or believe. KNOW.

## The Answer

The answer is obviously yes.

You don't need proof. You don't need evidence. You just know.

## The Nature of This Knowing

This knowing is not intellectual. It is self-evident.

It is awareness knowing itself.

## The Pointer

This knowing - this "I am" - is the doorway.

It is what you are before any description.

## Not "I am this or that"

Not "I am a person" or "I am a body."

Just: I am.

Pure being. Pure knowing.

## The Investigation

Rest in "I am."

What is this knowing? Where does it come from?

Who knows it?

## The Discovery

The knowing IS the known.

Awareness knows itself.

This is what you are.

## The Meditation

Simply rest in "I am."

Without adding anything.

What is that?
