# The Evening Review

## The Purpose

The evening review is a practice of reflection that helps integrate recognition into daily life.

It is not about self-improvement. It is about noticing how the day unfolded in awareness.

## The Practice

Before sleep, take a few minutes:

**1. Settle**
Let the activity of the day subside. Rest in simple presence.

**2. Review**
Let the day replay gently, not analyzing, just watching.

**3. Notice Identification**
Where did you get lost in the sense of being a separate self? Arguments, worries, desires?

**4. Notice Presence**
Were there moments of presence? Moments when you were simply aware without story?

**5. No Judgment**
This is not about doing it right. It's about seeing clearly.

**6. Rest**
Let go of the day. Let go of the review. Rest in awareness.

## The Benefit

Over time, this practice creates a natural awareness of patterns. You begin to notice identification as it happens, not just in retrospect.

## The Deeper Purpose

Ultimately, there is nothing to improve. The separate self is illusory - its successes and failures equally so.

The evening review is training in stepping back as awareness, not in becoming a better person.

## When You Forget

If you fall asleep without reviewing, that's fine. The practice is a pointer, not a requirement.

What you are doesn't need any practice.
