# Creativity and Non-Duality

## The Source

Where do creative insights come from? If we watch carefully, they arise from nothing. From the unknown. From emptiness.

We don't create them. They arise in us.

## The Paradox

The ego wants to claim creativity: "I thought of that." But the honest creator knows: it came through me, not from me.

This is not false modesty. It is accurate observation.

## The Practice

When creating - writing, painting, making music, coding, cooking - can you:

1. Set intention but release control?
2. Allow rather than force?
3. Not-know what will come?
4. Receive rather than generate?

## The Block

Creative block happens when the ego takes over. "I need to create something good." "People will judge this." "I don't know what to do."

These thoughts interrupt the natural flow from emptiness.

## The Flow

When creativity flows, there is no sense of separate self. The creation happens; the creator is absent.

Athletes call it "the zone." Artists call it "the muse." It is simply awareness expressing itself without the interference of ego.

## The Recognition

Creating from this place, we recognize: we are not the creators. We are the space in which creation happens.

This is simultaneously humbling (the ego is not in charge) and liberating (the pressure is off).

## The Invitation

Next time you create, try this: Instead of asking "What should I make?" ask "What wants to be made through me?"

Then listen.
