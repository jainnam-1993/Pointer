# The Wonder of Being

## The Mystery

Why is there something rather than nothing?

This is the ultimate mystery.

## The Fact

Yet here you are. Being. Aware.

Not understanding why. But undeniably being.

## The Wonder

When we stop taking existence for granted:

Wonder arises.

Not intellectual wonder. Felt wonder.

## The Ordinary

We make being ordinary. Background.

But it is the most extraordinary thing possible.

## The Recognition

Recognizing wonder:

Life becomes alive again.

The ordinary becomes extraordinary.

## The Practice

Can you feel the wonder of simply being?

Not understanding it. Feeling it.

## The Invitation

Before any thought about what you are:

You are.

Rest in that wonder.
