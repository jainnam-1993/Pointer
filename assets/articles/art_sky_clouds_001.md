# Sky and Clouds

## The Analogy

You are like the sky. Thoughts and experiences are like clouds.

Clouds appear in the sky. They pass through. The sky remains unchanged.

## The Application

Thoughts appear in awareness. Emotions pass through. Awareness remains unchanged.

You have never been the clouds. You are the sky.

## The Key Point

The sky is not affected by clouds.

Dark clouds don't make the sky dark. The sky is always clear.

Likewise, difficult thoughts don't disturb awareness. Awareness is always clear.

## Looking Up

When we look at the sky, we usually see clouds.

When we look at awareness, we usually see thoughts.

Look at the sky itself. Look at awareness itself.

## The Spaciousness

The sky holds all clouds without preference.

Awareness holds all experience without preference.

This is equanimity. This is peace.

## The Limitation

The analogy has limits. Sky and clouds are both objects.

Awareness is not an object. It is what knows objects.

But the pointing helps.

## The Practice

When thoughts feel dense, remember: You are the sky.

Look up, metaphorically. See the vastness that holds all.

Rest there.
