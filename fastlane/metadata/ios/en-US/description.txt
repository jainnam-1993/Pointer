Here Now offers short, direct pointings from the world's non-dual and contemplative traditions: invitations to notice what you already are, right now.

It is not a meditation course. There are no streaks, scores, or progress bars. Open it, read one line, and look.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Three calm tabs (Today, Library, Saved), with Settings one tap away
• Today: one pointing at a time, on a quote card, from Advaita Vedanta, Zen, the Direct Path, and contemporary teachers such as Ramana Maharshi, Nisargadatta Maharaj, and Rupert Spira
• Library: a grid of teachings and short articles, organized by tradition and teacher
• Lineages: explore the teachers behind each tradition
• Guided self-inquiry sessions that unfold one question at a time
• Saved: your favorites and recent history
• Gentle reminders at the times you choose
• Home-screen widget that takes its tone from your wallpaper and changes pointings without opening the app; Settings shows how to add it
• Share a pointing as a designed card or as text, or export it to your journal
• Calm design in sage and sand with a glass tab bar, light, dark, high-contrast, and true-black themes, large text support, and screen-reader labels
• Made for iPhone and iPad, with a side rail, two-pane screens, and keyboard shortcuts

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up
• No ads, analytics, or tracking
• Everything stays on your device; the app works fully offline

FREE, WITH OPTIONAL TIPS
Every feature is free. If Here Now helps you, you can leave an optional one-time tip in the app. Tips unlock nothing and are never required.
